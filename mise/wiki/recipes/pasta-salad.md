# Pasta Salad

A two-stage pasta salad: an oil-and-lemon base made ahead, with mayonnaise and raw onion folded in only at serving.

## Ingredients
- [[pasta]], cooked and cooled (quantity inferred; about 8 oz dry)
- [[zucchini]], diced
- [[sweet-corn]]
- [[bell-pepper]], diced
- [[carrot]], quick-boiled about 2 minutes; or broccoli
- [[broccoli]], quick-boiled about 2 minutes (optional)
- [[basil]], leaves, torn
- [[olive-oil]]
- [[lemon]], juiced
- [[black-pepper]], to taste
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste
- [[red-pepper-flakes]], to taste
- [[mayonnaise]], folded in at serving
- [[onion]], finely chopped, added at serving

## Method
1. Cook the pasta in a [[saucepan]], drain, and cool it.
2. Cut the vegetables. If you are using carrot or broccoli, boil it quickly — about 2 minutes — to soften it slightly, then drain and cool.
3. Mix the pasta and vegetables, then add the basil, olive oil, lemon juice, black pepper, salt and chili flakes, and toss.
4. **Only when you are ready to serve**, fold in the mayonnaise and the chopped onion.

## Notes
- **The storage warning is the point of the recipe:** the source says adding the mayo and onion and storing overnight makes it "watery and gross". Build and dress the oil-and-lemon base ahead, refrigerate, and fold in mayo and onion at the last minute.
- Carrot or broccoli gets a 2-minute boil only — softened slightly, still with bite.
- Basil goes in with the oil and lemon, not scattered at the end.
- **The source gives no quantities for anything, including the pasta**, and its "Servings | Prep Time | Total Time" header is an unfilled template. Servings and times are estimates built on a standard 8 oz dry batch.
- Carrot and broccoli read as an either/or, not both.
- The mayonnaise is what blocks vegan; wheat pasta is what blocks gluten-free.

### Scaling
- Scale by eye; the dressing is all to taste.
