# Tex-Mex Salad

A quinoa, black bean and corn bowl dressed with a lime-and-spice marinade, with avocado folded in at the last moment.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup [[quinoa]], uncooked, tri-coloured preferred
- 1 cup [[sweet-corn]], fresh, frozen or canned
- 1 cup [[black-beans]], drained and rinsed
- 1 cup [[red-cabbage]], thinly cut
- 1 [[bell-pepper]], large red, roughly chopped
- 2 [[avocado]], large, cubed just before serving
- 1/3 cup [[cilantro]], chopped
- 1 tbsp [[olive-oil]], or avocado oil (optional)
- 1 tsp [[coriander-cumin-powder]]
- 1/2 tsp [[chili-lime-seasoning]], adjust to taste
- 1/2 tsp [[all-purpose-seasoning]], 21 Seasoning Salute preferred
- 1 clove [[garlic]], minced
- 2 [[lime]], one juiced for the marinade, one in wedges to serve
- [[kosher-salt]], a pinch
- [[jalapeno]], sliced, to serve (optional)

## Method
1. Cook the quinoa in a [[saucepan]] — the source lists 1 cup quinoa to 1.5 cups water, and says to follow the packet. 1 cup dry makes about 3 cups cooked.
2. While it cooks, prep the vegetables: corn, drained black beans, thinly shredded red cabbage, chopped red pepper, chopped cilantro. Leave the avocado whole for now.
3. Whisk the oil, coriander-cumin powder, chili lime seasoning, all-purpose seasoning, minced garlic, a pinch of salt and the juice of one lime into a marinade.
4. Combine the quinoa and vegetables in a large bowl, pour the marinade over, and stir.
5. Just before serving, cube the avocados and fold them in.
6. Garnish with cilantro and lime wedges. Serve warm or chilled.

## Notes
- **Avocado goes in at the very last moment**, by design, so it doesn't brown. Everything else can be mixed ahead, which is what makes this a good potluck dish.
- The oil is explicitly optional — the source says it "can easily be skipped" for an oil-free salad.
- The dressing is a spice marinade carried by lime juice, not a vinaigrette.
- Yield cue from the source: 1 cup uncooked quinoa is about 3 cups cooked.
- [[coriander-cumin-powder]] is dhana jeera; equal parts ground coriander and ground cumin stand in.
- Internal tension in the source: the list gives 1.5 cups water to 1 cup quinoa, but step 1 says to follow the packet, which is usually 2:1.
- The asterisk after "Tri-Colored Quinoa" has no matching footnote anywhere on the page.
- Both seasoning blends are brand-dependent and vary in salt — taste before adding the full amount. Usually gluten-free, worth a label check.
- The jalapeño here is fresh, not the pickled jalapenos used elsewhere in this collection.
- Servings and both times are estimates; the source gives none.

### Scaling
- Scales linearly. Hold the avocado back if you are making it ahead.
