# Instant Pot Chana Masala

One-pot Punjabi chole in the [[instant-pot]]: a cumin tadka in ghee, onion-ginger-garlic and ground spices, chickpeas pressure-cooked in the masala, fresh tomato stirred in at the end. (per [[instant-pot-chana-masala-punjabi-chole]])

## Ingredients
- 1 cup [[dried-chickpeas]] (soaked in 2 cups warm water), or canned chickpeas (see Notes)
- 3½ cups water, divided (2 cups to soak, 1½ cups to cook)
- 2 tbsp [[ghee]] (or oil)
- 1 tsp [[cumin-seeds]]
- 1 [[onion]], finely diced
- 1 tsp grated [[ginger]] and 1 tbsp minced [[garlic]]
- 1 tbsp [[ground-coriander]], 2 tsp [[kosher-salt]], 1-2 tsp [[kashmiri-chili-powder]], ¼ tsp [[ground-turmeric]]
- 2 [[roma-tomato|plum tomatoes]], finely diced (or 1 cup canned tomatoes with juice)
- ¼ tsp [[garam-masala]]
- ½ cup chopped [[cilantro]]

## Method
1. Rinse the chickpeas and soak in 2 cups warm water for at least 4 hours or overnight. Drain.
2. On high sauté, heat the ghee. Add the cumin seeds and cook until they sizzle, about 1 minute ([[blooming-spices]]).
3. Add the onion and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. A glass lid speeds it up.
4. Add ginger and garlic; sauté 1 minute. Add the coriander, salt, chili powder, turmeric and chickpeas, then 1½ cups water. Stir well, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
5. Seal, press Cancel, then pressure cook on high for 35 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally ([[pressure-cooking]]).
6. Open, stir in the tomatoes and garam masala, and cook on high sauté until the tomatoes soften, about 5 minutes. Garnish with cilantro.
7. Serve with paratha or puri, or with jeera or basmati rice for gluten-free.

## Notes
- **Canned chickpeas** (makes it a weeknight dish): rinse and drain, use only ½ cup water in step 4, and pressure cook 5 minutes. About 2 cans replaces 1 cup dried. (per [[instant-pot-chana-masala-punjabi-chole]]; the can count is an estimate)
- Scraping the pot in step 4 also helps avoid the Instant Pot's burn warning.
- Ghee makes it vegetarian; use oil for vegan.
