# Pasta Aglio e Olio

Roman-style spaghetti with garlic, olive oil, and chile. Five pantry ingredients, so the technique decides how it turns out.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb [[spaghetti]]
- 1/2 cup [[olive-oil]]
- 6 cloves [[garlic]], thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp [[red-pepper-flakes]]
- 1/4 cup chopped [[parsley]]
- [[parmesan]] (optional, and not traditional)
- [[kosher-salt]] for the water

## Method
1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water, using less water than usual so it gets starchier. Pull it 2 minutes before al dente, and keep 1.5 cups of the water.
2. Meanwhile, put the garlic and oil in a cold [[skillet]] and heat over medium-low until the garlic is pale gold, 4–6 minutes. Add the pepper flakes for the last 30 seconds.
3. Add the pasta and 3/4 cup of the pasta water. Toss hard over medium-high until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the pasta ([[emulsifying-pasta-water]]). Add more water as needed.
4. Toss in the parsley off the heat.

## Notes
- If the garlic gets past golden it turns bitter, and you should start over. Starting in cold oil is what prevents that.
