# Methi Lagaan

A baked savoury cake of semolina, besan and greens — the oven-baked cousin of handvo. Attributed to Jyoti.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup [[rava]]
- 1/4 cup [[besan]]
- 1/4 cup [[all-purpose-flour]], or whole wheat flour; the source says maida tastes better
- 1/2 tsp [[baking-powder]]
- 1/2 tsp [[baking-soda]]
- 1 tsp [[sugar]]
- 1 tsp [[kosher-salt]], a starting point; the source says swad anusar, to taste
- 1/2 cup [[milk]]
- 1/2 cup [[neutral-oil]]
- 1 tsp [[green-chili]], very finely chopped
- 2-3 clove [[garlic]], grated
- 1/4 cup [[sweet-corn]], thawed if frozen
- 1/2 cup [[carrot]], grated
- 1 1/2 cup [[fenugreek-leaves]], finely chopped, fresh or frozen
- 1 [[onion]], small, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tbsp [[sesame-seeds]], roasted, split into two halves
- 1 tsp [[red-chili-powder]], or as needed

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a [[baking-dish]] well.
2. In a large bowl, mix the chopped methi, grated carrot, thawed corn, chopped onion, green chili and grated garlic.
3. Add the salt and the rava, and mix well.
4. Add the besan and the flour, and mix well.
5. Pour in the milk and the oil, and mix until the batter is consistent throughout.
6. Add half the roasted sesame seeds ([[dry-roasting]]), the sugar, the chili powder, and more salt if it needs it.
7. Add the baking soda and baking powder and mix — from here, get it into the oven promptly.
8. Pour into the greased pan and level it. Scatter the remaining sesame seeds over the top.
9. Bake 35-40 minutes. Serve hot, cut into squares, with chutney or ketchup.

## Notes
- **Attributed to Jyoti** in the source heading.
- Equal parts milk and oil, 1/2 cup each, is what keeps it moist — it is an unusually oil-rich batter.
- The vegetables are mixed with the salt and rava *before* the flours go in, so the semolina starts hydrating in the vegetables' own moisture.
- **Leaveners go in last**, right before the pan, so bake promptly after mixing.
- Sesame is deliberately split: half into the batter, half on top as a crust.
- The source offers palak as an equal alternative to the methi (the wiki's spinach page covers it).
- On flour: maida or wheat flour, but the source says it "tastes better with maida".
- Salt is given as "1 tsp salt (Swad Anusar)" — *swad anusar* means to taste, so 1 tsp is a starting point.
- Servings is an estimate from the pan and the photos, which show it cut into 8-9 squares; the source gives no yield.
- The source says only "chili powder"; [[red-chili-powder]] is the generic page. If the family means Kashmiri, the heat is much lower.

### Scaling
- It is a single-pan batter: double into a larger dish with a longer bake rather than two thin ones.
