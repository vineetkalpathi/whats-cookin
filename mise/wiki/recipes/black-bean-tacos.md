# Black Bean Tacos

Smashed, cumin-scented black beans in charred corn tortillas, with raw onion, cilantro, lime, and queso fresco.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp [[neutral-oil]]
- 1 small [[onion]], diced, plus some raw for garnish
- 2 cloves [[garlic]], minced
- 1 tsp ground [[cumin]]
- 2 cans [[black-beans]], drained (keep the liquid)
- 12 [[corn-tortillas]]
- 2 [[lime]]s, one juiced and one cut into wedges
- 1/4 cup [[cilantro]], 4 oz [[queso-fresco]], and [[kosher-salt]]

## Method
1. Soften the onion in the oil in a [[skillet]], 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cumin and cook 30 seconds ([[blooming-spices]]).
2. Add the beans and a splash of their liquid. Mash roughly and simmer until thick, then season with salt and the juice of one lime.
3. Char the tortillas ([[charring-tortillas]]) and keep them wrapped in a towel.
4. Fill the tortillas, then top with raw onion, cilantro, queso fresco, and lime wedges.
