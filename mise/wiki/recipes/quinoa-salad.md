# Quinoa Salad

A fridge-clearing chopped salad where quinoa is a minor component, dressed with a honey-mustard vinaigrette shaken in a bottle.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup [[quinoa]], cooked
- 1 [[zucchini]], diced (quantity inferred)
- 1/2 [[onion]], finely chopped (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[cucumber]], diced (quantity inferred)
- 2 stem [[celery]], sliced (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[carrot]], grated, raw or slightly cooked (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[bell-pepper]], diced (quantity inferred)
- 1 can [[chickpeas]], drained and rinsed
- 1 [[avocado]], diced (quantity inferred)
- 1/4 cup [[dried-cranberries]] (quantity inferred)
- 1/2 cup [[feta]], crumbled (quantity inferred)
- [[parsley]], chopped; or dill
- [[dill]], chopped (optional)
- 4 tbsp [[olive-oil]]
- 1 tbsp [[lemon]], juiced
- [[honey-mustard]], to taste
- [[ground-cumin]], to taste
- [[garlic-powder]], to taste
- [[red-pepper-flakes]], to taste
- [[black-pepper]], to taste
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste

## Method
1. Cook the quinoa — about 1/2 cup cooked is all you need — and let it cool.
2. Cut the vegetables to the size you like: zucchini, onion, cucumber, celery, peppers. Grate the carrot, raw or lightly cooked.
3. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Dice the avocado.
4. Combine the vegetables, chickpeas, cranberries, crumbled feta, cooled quinoa and chopped parsley or dill in a bowl.
5. Put the olive oil, lemon juice, honey mustard, cumin, garlic powder, salt, pepper and chili flakes into a bottle or jar, cap it, and shake until emulsified.
6. Pour over the salad and toss.

## Notes
- **This is a ratio recipe, not a measured one.** Apart from "around 1/2 cup cooked qinoa", "around 4 tbs" olive oil and "around 1 tbs" lemon juice, the source gives no quantities at all. Everything else here was inferred at ingest.
- **The source's "Servings | Prep Time | Total Time" header is an unfilled template** — the three words with no values. Servings and times here are estimates.
- The dressing is shaken in a bottle, so it can be made ahead.
- Parsley and dill are alternatives, not both.
- Carrot can go in raw-grated or lightly cooked — the source offers either.
- Dried cranberries and feta are the sweet-salty hook that separates this from a plain salad.
- The honey mustard is what blocks a vegan tag; swap it for plain mustard and maple, and drop the feta.
- The source spells it "QINOA" throughout.
- Add the avocado near serving so it doesn't brown — the wiki's advice, not the source's.
