# Chana Masala (Traditional Chole)

Restaurant-style Punjabi chole: dried chickpeas pressure-cooked dark with a tea bag, then simmered in a ghee-browned onion-tomato masala and finished with kasoori methi. Serve with basmati rice or paratha. (per [[traditional-chana-masala-recipe-chole]])

## Ingredients
**Chickpeas**
- 1 cup [[dried-chickpeas]], plus 2 cups water to soak and 2 cups to cook
- 1 tsp [[kosher-salt]]
- 1 [[black-tea]] bag (optional, for color and a smoky note)

**Masala**
- 2 tbsp [[ghee]] (or oil)
- 1 [[bay-leaf]]
- 1 large [[onion]], finely diced
- 2 tsp [[ginger]] paste and 2 tsp [[garlic]] paste
- 1 [[green-chili]], slit (optional)
- 2 [[roma-tomato|plum tomatoes]], finely diced
- 1-2 tbsp [[chana-masala-powder|chana masala spice blend]]
- 1½ tsp [[kosher-salt]], ¼ tsp [[ground-turmeric]], 1 tsp [[kashmiri-chili-powder]]
- About 1 cup water
- 1 tbsp [[kasoori-methi]] and ¼ cup chopped [[cilantro]], to finish

## Method
1. Soak the chickpeas in 2 cups of water overnight, 8-10 hours. Drain.
2. Put the chickpeas, 2 cups fresh water, the salt and the tea bag (if using) in the [[instant-pot]] or a stovetop pressure cooker. Pressure cook 35 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally ([[pressure-cooking]]). Discard the tea bag; keep the cooking liquid.
3. Meanwhile, heat the ghee in a medium [[skillet]]. Add the bay leaf ([[blooming-spices]]) and onions and sauté over medium heat until light golden brown, 8-10 minutes, stirring often. A lid speeds this up.
4. Add the ginger and garlic pastes and the green chili; cook 1 minute.
5. Add the tomatoes and cook about 5 minutes, stirring a few times, until soft and mushy.
6. Stir in the chana masala blend, salt, turmeric and chili powder. Add the chickpeas with their cooking water, plus about 1 cup water to reach the consistency you like.
7. Simmer 5 minutes. Garnish with kasoori methi (crush it between your palms) and cilantro.

## Notes
- Total time is mostly the overnight soak. With canned chickpeas, skip steps 1-2: rinse 2 cans of chickpeas and simmer them in the masala with water (the Instant Pot version from the same site gives canned guidance; see [[instant-pot-chana-masala]]).
- Most of the flavor comes from the store-bought blend, so the brand matters. Add more blend to taste.
- Ghee makes it vegetarian rather than vegan; use oil for vegan.
- Replaces an earlier seed version (general knowledge) of this page.
