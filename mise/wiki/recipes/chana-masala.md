# Chana Masala

Punjabi-style chickpea curry in a spiced onion-tomato *masala*. A pantry-friendly weeknight main. Serve with rice or flatbread.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp [[neutral-oil]] (or ghee)
- 1 tsp [[cumin-seeds]]
- 1/8 tsp [[asafoetida]]
- 1 large [[onion]], finely chopped
- 4 cloves [[garlic]], minced, and 1 inch [[ginger]], grated
- 1 can (14.5 oz) [[crushed-tomatoes]]
- 2 tsp [[garam-masala]]
- 2 cans [[chickpeas]], drained
- 1 tsp [[kosher-salt]], plus more to taste
- Juice of 1/2 [[lime]], and 1/4 cup chopped [[cilantro]]

## Method
1. Heat the oil in the [[dutch-oven]] over medium-high. Add the cumin seeds and fry until they darken a shade and crackle, about 30 seconds, then stir in the asafoetida ([[blooming-spices]]).
2. Add the onion and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring, until deep golden, 10–12 minutes. Don't rush this step: the browned onion is the base of the dish.
3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook 1 minute. Add the tomatoes, half the garam masala, and the salt. Cook until the oil separates at the edges, about 8 minutes.
4. Add the chickpeas and 1 cup of water. Simmer 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken the sauce.
5. Stir in the rest of the garam masala, then the lime juice. Taste for salt, and garnish with cilantro.

## Notes
- "Oil separating from the masala" is the cue that the tomato has cooked out. It's used all over North Indian cooking.
