# Asparagus Soup

Blended asparagus soup with a tadka-style start — garlic, green chili, onion — and a teaspoon of flour to thicken.

## Ingredients
- 1 bunch [[asparagus]], stem ends discarded, each spear cut into 4 (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[onion]], medium, chopped
- 5-6 clove [[garlic]], chopped fine-ish
- 1 [[green-chili]], chopped
- 2-3 tbsp [[neutral-oil]]
- 1 tsp [[all-purpose-flour]]
- 2 cup [[vegetable-broth]], topped up with water if it needs more liquid
- 1 tbsp [[vegetable-bouillon-paste]], with 2 cups water in place of the broth (optional)
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste
- [[milk]], to finish, to taste

## Method
1. Trim the asparagus and cut each spear into 4 pieces.
2. Chop the onion roughly, the garlic fairly fine, and the green chili.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan, or in the [[instant-pot]] on sauté.
4. Add the garlic; after 2-3 seconds, once it sizzles, add the green chili, then the onion ([[blooming-spices]]).
5. When the onion turns translucent, sprinkle in the flour and cook about a minute, stirring, until it is blended in and no longer raw.
6. Add the asparagus and stir to coat.
7. Add either 2 cups vegetable broth, or 2 cups water plus a big tablespoon of bouillon paste.
8. Salt carefully — the broth is already salted.
9. Boil until the asparagus is tender.
10. Blend smooth in a [[blender]], then add milk to loosen and enrich it.

## Notes
- **The aromatic order is a tadka grafted onto a Western cream soup:** garlic first and only 2-3 seconds, then chili, then onion.
- Cook the flour for about a minute before any liquid goes in, or the soup tastes of raw flour. At 1 tsp to 2 cups it is a light thickener, not a roux.
- Milk goes in at the end and never boils.
- Broth versus water-plus-bouillon is explicitly the cook's choice; salt accordingly.
- **The source gives no asparagus quantity, no milk quantity, no yield and no times.** The bunch, the servings and both times here are all inferred.
- The source says to discard "soft tips/hard stem edges", which is unusual — most cooks keep the tips. Transcribed as written rather than silently corrected.
- Finish with a plant milk and it is vegan; the rest of the recipe is already dairy-free.

### Scaling
- Scale the broth with however much asparagus you have, keeping the liquid just covering the vegetables.
