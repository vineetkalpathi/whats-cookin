# Thai Curry

A loose family template: fry the aromatics in order, fry the curry paste, then vegetables, tofu and coconut.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp [[neutral-oil]] (quantity inferred)
- 2 [[green-chili]], cut (quantity inferred)
- 4 clove [[garlic]], grated (quantity inferred)
- 1 inch [[ginger]], grated (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[onion]], cut (quantity inferred)
- 2 tbsp [[mint]], cut (quantity inferred)
- 1/2 tsp [[ground-cumin]]
- 1/2 tsp [[black-pepper]]
- 2 tbsp [[thai-red-curry-paste]], red or green (quantity inferred)
- 2 cup [[broccoli]], cut into florets (quantity inferred)
- 2 cup [[cauliflower]], cut into florets (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[bell-pepper]], sliced (quantity inferred)
- 1 [[zucchini]], sliced (quantity inferred)
- 8 oz [[mushroom]], sliced (quantity inferred)
- 1 cup [[baby-corn]], cut (quantity inferred)
- 14 oz [[extra-firm-tofu]], cubed (quantity inferred)
- 13.5 oz [[coconut-milk]], 1 can (quantity inferred)
- 1/2 cup [[coconut-cream]] (quantity inferred)
- 1 tbsp [[soy-sauce]], when making a larger quantity (optional)
- 2 tbsp [[cilantro]], leaves, to finish (quantity inferred)
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste
- [[red-chili-powder]], to taste (optional)

## Method
1. For a large volume of hard vegetables — broccoli, cauliflower, pepper, zucchini — pre-cook them slightly over medium-high heat with a little salt. For a small volume, skip this and add them raw at step 3 ([[saute-to-bite]]).
2. Heat oil in a [[saucepan]] over high heat and sauté **in this order**: green chili, then garlic and ginger, then onion, then mint. Add the ground cumin and pepper. Once the mixture looks cooked and shiny, add the curry paste and a little water ([[frying-curry-paste]]).
3. Add the vegetables and stir so they soak in the gravy. Add red chili powder here if you want more heat.
4. Add the cubed tofu, cover, and let it boil on high for a few minutes.
5. Add the coconut milk and coconut cream and cook a little longer.
6. Scatter cilantro over the top and serve.

## Notes
> [!warning] Most quantities are inferred
> The source gives amounts for only three things: 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp pepper and 1 tbsp soy sauce. Every other quantity on this page was inferred at ingest and scaled to 4 servings. Treat them as a starting point, not as the family's recipe.
- **The aromatic order is the opinionated part:** chili, then garlic and ginger, then onion, then mint — the source says "put in that order".
- Doneness cue for the aromatics: they are ready when they look "cooked/shiny", which is when the paste goes in.
- The 1 tbsp soy sauce is explicitly "for larger quantity" — a seasoning adjustment when scaling up, not a base ingredient.
- Coconut milk *and* coconut cream both go in, at the end, after the tofu.
- Oil and red chili powder are used in the method but missing from the source's ingredient list.
- Green curry paste is an equal alternative to red.
- **Not tagged vegan:** many curry pastes contain shrimp paste or fish sauce. Check the jar. Not gluten-free as written because of the soy sauce.
- Tofu firmness is unspecified in the source; extra-firm is the wiki's assumption.

### Scaling
- The source's own scaling rule: pre-cook the hard vegetables separately only when the volume is high; at low volume they go in raw.
- Add the 1 tbsp soy sauce when making a larger batch.
