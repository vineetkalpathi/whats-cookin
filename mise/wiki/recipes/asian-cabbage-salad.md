# Asian Cabbage Salad

A two-cabbage chopped slaw with edamame and toasted almonds. The captured page is missing its dressing recipe.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 head [[green-cabbage]], small, thinly sliced
- 1/2 head [[red-cabbage]], small, thinly sliced
- 1 [[bell-pepper]], red, thinly sliced (quantity inferred)
- 2 cup [[carrot]], shredded (quantity inferred)
- 1 1/2 cup [[edamame]], shelled, thawed (quantity inferred)
- 1/2 cup [[cilantro]], chopped (quantity inferred)
- 1 cup [[almonds]], sliced, toasted (quantity inferred)
- 3/4 cup [[ginger-peanut-dressing]], to taste (quantity inferred; recipe missing from the source)

## Method
1. Toast the almonds in a dry [[skillet]] until fragrant and lightly golden. Stay at the pan — they turn from toasted to burnt in seconds ([[toasting-nuts]]).
2. Slice the green and red cabbage very thinly.
3. Put the cabbage, red pepper, shredded carrot, edamame and cilantro in a large bowl.
4. Make the ginger peanut dressing. **The source does not give this recipe.**
5. Pour the dressing over a little at a time, tossing as you go, until the salad is dressed to your taste.
6. Scatter the toasted almonds over the top, toss once more, and serve.
