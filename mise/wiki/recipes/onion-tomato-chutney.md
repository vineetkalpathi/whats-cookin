# Onion Tomato Chutney

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp [[neutral-oil]]
- 1 tbsp [[urad-dal]]
- 1 tbsp [[chana-dal]]
- 3-4 [[dried-red-chili]]
- 1 [[onion]], finely chopped
- 2 clove [[garlic]]
- 2 [[tomato]], finely chopped
- [[tamarind]], a small piece
- [[kosher-salt]], as needed

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a [[skillet]] over medium heat.
2. Fry the urad dal and chana dal until golden and nutty, then add the dried red chilies for a few seconds more ([[blooming-spices]]).
3. Add the garlic and onion and cook until the onion softens and turns translucent.
4. Add the tomatoes and the tamarind, and cook until the tomatoes break down and the mixture thickens.
5. Salt, cool fully, then blend to a coarse or smooth chutney, loosening with a splash of water.

## Notes
- **The fried dals are the structural trick** of this style: urad and chana dal give the chutney body and a roasted flavour that raw aromatics can't.
- [[tamarind]] is the sourness; there is no other acid in it.
- Quantities are small and clearly meant to scale by eye — the salt and the chili count are both "as needed".
- Servings is an estimate; the source gives none.
