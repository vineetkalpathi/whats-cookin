# Paneer Kathi Rolls

Paneer and peppers in a yogurt-and-spice marinade, cooked fast and rolled into frozen paratha with two chutneys.

## Ingredients
- 2 cup [[paneer]], cut in 1 inch cubes
- 1/4 cup [[plain-yogurt]]
- 1 tsp [[ginger]], grated
- 1 tsp [[garlic]], pressed (the filling uses more, unquantified in the source)
- 1-2 tsp [[kashmiri-chili-powder]]
- 1 tsp [[garam-masala]]
- 1/4 tsp [[ground-turmeric]]
- 1 tsp [[ground-cumin]], roasted
- 1 tsp [[ground-coriander]]
- 1 tsp [[amchur]]
- 1 tbsp [[kasoori-methi]]
- 1 [[green-chili]], sliced
- 1 tsp [[kosher-salt]], for the marinade
- 1-2 [[bell-pepper]], mixed colours, thinly sliced
- 1 [[red-onion]], medium, thinly sliced, some kept raw for assembly
- 2 tbsp [[neutral-oil]], for the filling
- 1/2 tsp [[kosher-salt]], for the filling
- 6-8 [[paratha]], Kawan or any brand
- 1/2 cup [[cilantro]], chopped
- [[green-chutney]], to serve
- [[sweet-chutney]], to serve

## Method
1. Quickly sauté the sliced green chili with the grated ginger and pressed garlic.
2. Mix all the marinade ingredients — yogurt, ginger, garlic, Kashmiri chili, garam masala, turmeric, roasted cumin, coriander, amchur, kasoori methi, green chili and 1 tsp salt — in a bowl. Add the paneer cubes and mix well.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a [[skillet]]. Add garlic, the sliced onion, the peppers and 1/2 tsp salt, and cook 3-4 minutes, keeping them crisp ([[saute-to-bite]]).
4. Add the marinated paneer, mix, and cook another 3-4 minutes. Turn off the heat and stir through the chopped cilantro.
5. Cook the parathas according to the packet.
6. Spread chutney on a paratha, lay 2-3 spoonfuls of filling down the middle, top with raw sliced onion and cilantro, and roll.

## Notes
- **The yogurt carries the whole masala.** Everything is mixed into 1/4 cup of yogurt, which coats the paneer and keeps the spices from catching in the pan.
- [[amchur]] (dried mango powder) is the sourness here, working with the yogurt.
- **Keep the pan time short.** 3-4 minutes for the vegetables, 3-4 more with the paneer — long enough to set the marinade, not long enough to toughen the cheese.
- Hold back some of the sliced red onion raw; the assembly step calls for it in the roll.
- The source gives no marinating time — it reads as mix-and-use-immediately.
- **Garlic appears in the filling step but is not in the source's ingredient list**, and has no quantity. The listed 1 tsp is the marinade's; add more to the pan by eye.
- Both chutneys are treated as bought or already made; no recipes or quantities are given.
- Both times are estimates — the source states only the 3-4 minute pan stages.

### Scaling
- Scales per roll: about 1 paratha and 2-3 spoonfuls of filling each.
