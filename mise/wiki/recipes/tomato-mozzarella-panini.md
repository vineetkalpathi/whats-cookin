# Tomato-Mozzarella Panini

A pressed caprese-style sandwich with the household twist: green chutney instead of pesto, and pickled jalapeños.

## Ingredients
- [[ciabatta]], split; or torta rolls, or any thick bread
- [[tomato]], sliced into rounds
- [[fresh-mozzarella]], sliced into rounds
- [[pickled-jalapenos]], sliced
- [[butter]], softened, for the outsides of the bread
- [[pesto]], for the insides; or green chutney
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste
- [[black-pepper]], to taste
- [[red-pepper-flakes]], to taste

## Method
1. Slice the tomatoes and the mozzarella into rounds of similar thickness.
2. Slice the jalapeños if they aren't already pickled.
3. Split the bread. Butter the **outside** faces and spread pesto or green chutney on the **inside** faces.
4. Layer tomato, mozzarella and jalapeños on the bottom half.
5. Sprinkle salt, black pepper and chili flakes over the filling.
6. Close the sandwich and toast it in a [[panini-press]] until the bread is crisp and the mozzarella has softened.
7. Cut into halves to serve.

## Notes
- **Butter outside, chutney or pesto inside** — the source is specific, and it is what gives the crisp grill-marked crust.
- Green chutney in place of pesto is what makes this an Indian-household panini rather than a straight caprese.
- The seasoning goes directly on the filling before closing, not on the bread.
- **The source gives no quantities at all, and no times** — it is a build, not a measured recipe. Servings comes from the photo: one ciabatta loaf, cut into two sandwiches.
- No panini press? A heavy skillet over medium heat with a second heavy pan pressing down does the same job.
- The source photographs it with a jar of [[tomato-soup]] alongside.

### Scaling
- Everything is per sandwich; one ciabatta loaf makes two.
