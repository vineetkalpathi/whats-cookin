# Killu Bonda

Deep-fried vegetable fritters on a four-flour batter bound with idli batter. The source gives ingredients only — the method here is reconstructed.

> [!warning] The method is reconstructed
> [[ammas-killu-bonda]] lists ingredients only - it gives no steps for this recipe, and the
> rest of the page is blank, so nothing was lost in capture. The method below was written
> from general cooking knowledge, **not** from the family's own instructions. Confirm it
> before trusting it; queued in `mise/inbox.md`.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup [[whole-wheat-flour]]
- 1/4 cup [[all-purpose-flour]]
- 1/4 cup [[rice-flour]]
- 1/3 cup [[besan]]
- 2 scoop [[idli-batter]], about 2/3 cup; or 1/3 cup more all-purpose flour plus 1/3 cup more rice flour
- 1/2 cup [[green-cabbage]], shredded
- 1/2 cup [[carrot]], shredded
- 1/3 cup [[bell-pepper]], finely cut
- 1 [[onion]], finely chopped
- 1 inch [[ginger]], grated
- 1 tbsp [[green-chili]], finely cut, or as needed
- [[asafoetida]], a pinch
- [[cilantro]], finely chopped
- [[curry-leaves]], finely chopped
- [[kosher-salt]], as needed
- 1/2 tsp [[baking-soda]], or eno salt
- [[neutral-oil]], for deep-frying

## Method
1. Combine the wheat flour, all-purpose flour, rice flour and besan in a bowl.
2. Add the shredded cabbage and carrot, the cut bell pepper, chopped onion, grated ginger, green chili, asafoetida, cilantro, curry leaves and salt.
3. Mix in the idli batter (or the extra flours), adding water a little at a time, to a thick batter that holds its shape rather than pours.
4. Rest briefly, then stir in the baking soda or eno just before frying.
5. Heat oil for [[deep-frying]] in a [[dutch-oven]] to about 350°F.
6. Pinch off or spoon in small portions, a few at a time without crowding, and fry until deep golden and crisp.
7. Drain and serve hot with [[onion-tomato-chutney]].

## Notes
- The source offers a substitution it clearly cares about: 2 scoops of idli batter, *or* 1/3 cup all-purpose flour plus 1/3 cup rice flour. The idli-batter version is fermented and tangier.
- [[rice-flour]] is what makes these crisp rather than bready.
- Baking soda and eno salt are given as interchangeable.
- **The source gives one combined measure, "4/3 cup shredded cabbage, carrot, finely cut bell pepper".** The split into 1/2 + 1/2 + 1/3 cup here is the wiki's, and sums to the same total.
- "Killu" refers to pinching off the portions by hand, which is where the name comes from.
- Yield is an estimate; the source gives none.

### Scaling
- Fry in small batches whatever the quantity — crowding drops the oil temperature and the bondas go greasy.
