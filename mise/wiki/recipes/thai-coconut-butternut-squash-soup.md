# Thai Coconut Curry Butternut Squash Soup

Blended butternut squash soup built on fried red curry paste, finished off the heat with coconut milk and lime. Stovetop or slow cooker.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp [[neutral-oil]], for the stovetop method
- 2 clove [[garlic]], minced
- 1 [[onion]], small yellow, diced
- 1 tsp [[ginger]], freshly grated
- 2 tbsp [[thai-red-curry-paste]]
- 2 cup [[vegetable-broth]], or chicken broth
- 1 [[butternut-squash]], medium, peeled, seeded, cut into 1-inch cubes (about 4 cups)
- 15 oz [[coconut-milk]], 1 can, divided
- 1 [[lime]], juiced
- 1/2 tsp [[kosher-salt]], plus more to taste
- 1/4 tsp [[black-pepper]], freshly ground
- 1/3 cup [[cilantro]], chopped, to garnish
- 1/3 cup [[peanuts]], unsalted dry-roasted, chopped, to garnish
- [[sriracha]], to taste (optional)
- 1 [[naan]], to serve (optional)

## Method
1. **Stovetop:** heat the oil in a [[dutch-oven]] over medium. Add the garlic and onion and sauté until soft and fragrant, about 3 minutes.
2. Add the ginger and curry paste and cook 3 more minutes, stirring often ([[frying-curry-paste]]).
3. Add the raw squash cubes, pour in the broth, and season with the salt and pepper.
4. Boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook 20 minutes until the squash is tender. Take it off the heat and let it cool a few minutes.
5. **Slow cooker instead:** put the squash, garlic, onion, ginger, curry paste, broth, salt and pepper straight into the [[slow-cooker]] with no oil. Cook on low 6-8 hours or high 3-4 hours.
6. **Both:** blend smooth, in batches in a [[blender]] or with an immersion blender in the pot.
7. Off the heat, stir in the coconut milk — holding back a few tablespoons — and the lime juice.
8. Season, and stir in sriracha for more heat if you want it.
9. Ladle out, drizzle the reserved coconut milk on top and swirl it, then scatter cilantro and chopped peanuts. Serve with naan.

## Notes
- **The coconut milk is divided on purpose:** the bulk goes in off the heat after blending so it doesn't split or dull, and a few tablespoons are saved back purely to drizzle and swirl on top.
- The oil is for the stovetop version only — omit it entirely in the slow cooker.
- The squash goes in raw either way; there is no roasting step.
- Lime juice goes in at the very end, off the heat.
- **Not tagged vegan:** many red curry pastes contain shrimp paste. The recipe also offers chicken broth, so the vegetable-broth choice is what keeps it vegetarian.
- Servings and both times are estimates; the source gives only the cooking durations.

### Scaling
- The slow cooker route is the batch-friendly one: 6-8 hours on low, hands-off.
