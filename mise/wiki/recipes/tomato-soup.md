# Tomato Soup

Tomato soup thickened with dry-roasted yellow moong dal instead of cream — made in batch and bottled.

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup [[yellow-moong-dal]]
- 3 [[tomato]], medium, cut
- 1 [[green-chili]], or black peppercorns, for spice
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste
- 1/2 tsp [[ghee]], to finish (optional)
- [[ground-cumin]], roasted, to finish (optional)

## Method
1. Dry roast the moong dal in a [[skillet]] for 2-3 minutes, until it smells nutty ([[dry-roasting]]).
2. Rinse and wash the dal **after** roasting.
3. Return it to the skillet with 2 cups of water and a little salt, and boil until the dal softens.
4. When the dal is about three-quarters soft, add the cut tomatoes.
5. Add the green chili or peppercorns and boil until both the tomatoes and the dal are fully cooked.
6. Keep topping up with water so everything stays submerged.
7. Let it cool, then blend smooth.
8. Adjust consistency with water and taste for salt.
9. Optionally finish each bowl with 1/2 tsp ghee and a sprinkle of roasted cumin.

## Notes
- **The dal is the body** — there is no cream and no roux. Dry roasting it first, for aroma, is the step the source singles out and explains.
- **Rinse the dal after roasting, not before**, or it won't toast.
- Tomatoes go in only when the dal is three-quarters soft, so they don't collapse before the dal is done.
- Liquid is managed by topping up rather than by a fixed ratio; the final consistency is set at the end.
- Cool before blending — the source says so explicitly, which matters with a jar blender.
- The yield is given as "3 bottles worth", so `yield_unit` is bottles; the photos show it jarred for the fridge.
- Leaving off the ghee makes it vegan.

### Scaling
- Scales freely — the water is adjusted by feel anyway.
