# Almond Flour Cake

A flourless almond sponge leavened with whipped egg whites, with a layer of chocolate chips through the middle.

## Ingredients
- 4 [[egg]], large, separated, at room temperature
- 1/2 cup [[sugar]], caster, for the batter
- 2 tbsp [[sugar]], caster, for coating the pan
- 1 tsp [[vanilla-extract]]
- 1 1/2 cup [[almond-flour]]
- 1 tsp [[baking-powder]]
- 1/4 tsp [[kosher-salt]]
- [[chocolate-chips]], a handful
- [[butter]], melted, for greasing the pan

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease an 8-inch round [[cake-pan]] with melted butter, then sprinkle 2 tbsp of the sugar over the bottom.
2. Beat the egg yolks, 1/4 cup of the sugar and the vanilla together until smooth.
3. With an [[electric-mixer]], whip the egg whites to soft peaks, then slowly beat in the remaining 1/4 cup sugar. Set aside.
4. Combine the almond flour, baking powder and salt, add to the yolk mixture, and stir to a thick dough.
5. Fold the whites into the dough 1/2 cup at a time, fully incorporating each addition, until the batter is smooth and fluffy ([[folding-egg-whites]]).
6. Pour half the batter into the pan, scatter the chocolate chips over the middle, then pour the rest on top.
7. Bake on the centre rack for 30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
8. Cool in the pan 5 minutes, run a knife around the edge, and turn out onto a plate.
9. Cool fully before topping with ganache, Nutella or sliced fruit — or eat it warm with tea.

## Notes
- **The sugar splits three ways:** 2 tbsp coats the buttered pan, 1/4 cup goes into the yolks, 1/4 cup into the whites. The sugar on the pan becomes a crisp crust on top once the cake is inverted.
- **The chocolate chips are a layer in the middle**, not stirred through — that is the one structural trick here.
- Expect a genuinely thick dough before the whites go in. That is correct; the whites loosen it.
- Doneness: golden brown and a clean toothpick at 30 minutes.
