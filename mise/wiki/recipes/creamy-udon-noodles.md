# Creamy Udon Noodles with Tofu

Chewy udon in a creamy silken-tofu and chili crisp sauce, topped with browned, soy-glazed tofu crumbles, scallions, cilantro and sesame. A vegan noodle bowl with two tofu textures. (per [[creamy-udon-noodles-with-tofu-high-protein]])

## Ingredients
**Creamy sauce**
- 10.6 oz [[silken-tofu]], drained
- 1 tbsp light [[soy-sauce]]
- 1 tbsp unseasoned [[rice-vinegar]]
- 1 tsp [[sugar]]
- 1-2 tbsp vegan [[chili-crisp]]

**Tofu crumbles**
- 10.6 oz [[extra-firm-tofu]], drained and patted dry
- 1 tbsp [[neutral-oil]]

**Glaze**
- 1 tbsp light [[soy-sauce]], 1 tsp [[dark-soy-sauce]]
- 1 tbsp [[tomato-paste]], 1 tsp [[sugar]], 3 tbsp water
- 1 tbsp [[nutritional-yeast]], ½ tsp [[garlic-powder]], ¼ tsp [[smoked-paprika]], ¼ tsp [[black-pepper]] (optional)

**Noodles & toppings**
- 14.1 oz precooked [[udon-noodles]] (weight before heating)
- 2 [[scallion|scallions]], thinly sliced; ¼ cup chopped [[cilantro]]; 2 tsp [[sesame-seeds]]
- Reserved noodle cooking water, as needed

## Method
1. Drain the silken tofu. Pat the extra-firm tofu dry and crumble it into small, uneven pieces. Put a large pot of water on for the noodles.
2. **Sauce:** in a [[blender]], blend the silken tofu, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar and 1 tbsp chili crisp until completely smooth. Taste; blend in more chili crisp for more heat.
3. **Crumbles:** heat the oil in a wide nonstick [[skillet]] over medium-high. Spread out the tofu and cook 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally and letting it sit between stirs, until the moisture is gone and it has golden patches. Lower the heat if it browns too fast.
4. **Glaze:** whisk the glaze ingredients together, pour over the tofu and cook over medium heat 3-5 minutes, stirring often, until reduced and coating. Take it off the heat before it burns.
5. **Noodles:** heat the udon per the package, loosening the strands. Reserve ½ cup (120 ml) cooking water and drain. Don't rinse.
6. Return the empty pot to low heat, add the sauce and stir until warm, 1-2 minutes, without a hard boil. Toss in the udon, adding noodle water a tablespoon at a time only if needed. Taste with a bit of the topping before adjusting the seasoning.
7. Divide between two bowls, top with the tofu, scallions, cilantro and sesame. Serve immediately.

## Notes
- The source calls it "Asian inspired". It's filed under [[japanese]] for udon, but the chili crisp is Chinese and the glaze (tomato paste, nutritional yeast, smoked paprika) is Western-vegan.
- "Light soy sauce" is regular Chinese-style thin soy sauce, not low-sodium. Dark soy is mainly for color and a molasses note.
