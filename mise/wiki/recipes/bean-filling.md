# Bean Filling

Amma's make-ahead bean and vegetable filling, chopped to bean size so every spoonful carries both. Keeps in the fridge and becomes quesadillas, enchiladas, or Mexican pasta.

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp [[neutral-oil]]
- 5 cloves [[garlic]], chopped
- 1 medium [[onion]], chopped
- Vegetables of your choice, all chopped to bean size - typically 0.5-1 [[bell-pepper]], 1 medium crown [[broccoli]], 1 [[zucchini]], 1 [[carrot]], 1 small carton [[mushroom]]
- 1/4 cup [[pickled-jalapenos]], plus 2-3 tbsp of the jar vinegar
- 1 can (15 oz) [[black-beans]], drained and rinsed (or pinto beans - see Notes)
- ~1 tsp roasted [[ground-cumin]], [[red-pepper-flakes]], [[sriracha]], [[black-pepper]]
- ~1 tsp [[taco-seasoning]] (optional)
- ~5 stems [[cilantro]], chopped

## Method
1. Cut and wash the vegetables. Chop everything to roughly bean size. Keep the onion and garlic separate from the rest.
2. Drain the beans in a colander and rinse until the canning liquid is gone.
3. Heat the oil in a [[skillet]]. Add the garlic; after 3-5 seconds add the onion.
4. Once the onion is slightly translucent, add the washed vegetables and cook to the bite you want ([[saute-to-bite]]).
5. Add the beans and the pickled jalapenos, along with 2-3 spoons of the jalapeno jar vinegar.
6. Add the roasted cumin, black pepper, crushed red pepper, sriracha, and taco seasoning if using. Season to taste.
7. Off the heat, stir through the chopped cilantro.
8. Cool and store in the fridge, for quesadillas, enchiladas, or Mexican pasta.

## Notes
- The source offers **pinto beans** as an equal alternative to black beans; they are softer and break down more, so add them a touch later. See [[black-beans]].
- **Chop to bean size.** This is the whole idea - the vegetables and beans should eat as one thing, not as vegetables with beans in them (per [[ammas-bean-filling]]).
- **The jalapeno jar vinegar is an ingredient**, not a leftover. It is the acid in the dish.
- **Texture is yours to set:** high heat and uncovered evaporates water fast and keeps the vegetables crunchy; lower heat, or a lid on, keeps water in and softens them.
- Spices go in at the end, once the vegetables are cooked.
- The vegetable list is a template. The original says "other veggies of your choice, like..." - what is written here is the usual set.
- `servings: 6` is an estimate from the quantities (one 15 oz can of beans plus about 5 cups of vegetables); the source gives no yield. Treat it as roughly enough for 6 quesadillas.
- `rotation: false` - this is a component, so it shouldn't surface as a weekly suggestion on its own.
