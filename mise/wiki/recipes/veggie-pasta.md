# Veggie Pasta

Amma's "pasta gravy": a pan of sauteed vegetables cooked to the bite you want, loosened with jarred sauce and tossed through pasta. A template more than a recipe - the pasta, the sauce, and the vegetables all change.

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp [[neutral-oil]]
- 5 cloves [[garlic]], chopped
- 1 medium [[onion]], chopped
- Vegetables of your choice, all chopped to bite size - typically 0.5-1 [[bell-pepper]], 1 medium crown [[broccoli]], 1 [[zucchini]], 1 [[carrot]], 1 small carton [[mushroom]]
- [[pickled-jalapenos]] (optional)
- 1 jar [[pasta-sauce]] - red, pesto, whatever you like
- 1 lb [[pasta]]
- [[red-pepper-flakes]], [[black-pepper]], [[kosher-salt]]
- [[basil]], chopped, to finish

## Method
1. Cut and wash the vegetables, chopping them to bite size. Keep the onion and garlic separate from the rest.
2. Heat the oil in a [[skillet]]. Add the garlic; after 3-5 seconds add the onion.
3. Once the onion is slightly translucent, add the washed vegetables and cook to the bite you want ([[saute-to-bite]]).
4. Season with black pepper, crushed red pepper, and salt, to taste. Stir in the pasta sauce and warm it through.
5. Cook the pasta.
6. Mix the pasta into the vegetables, finish with chopped basil, and serve.

## Notes
- **A template recipe.** The source says vegetables "of your choice" and "pasta sauce of your choice (red, pesto, etc.)". What's constant is the saute: oil, garlic, onion, then everything else (per [[ammas-pasta-gravy]]).
- **Texture is yours to set:** high heat and uncovered for crunch, lower heat or a lid for softer vegetables.
- Bite size here, not the bean size used in [[bean-filling]].
- Basil goes in off the heat.
- `servings: 4` is an estimate from 1 lb of pasta plus about 5 cups of vegetables; the source gives no yield.
- Filed as `veggie-pasta` rather than the original title "Pasta Gravy", at the user's request.
- Marked `vegetarian` not `vegan` because jarred red sauces and pesto often contain cheese - check the jar.
