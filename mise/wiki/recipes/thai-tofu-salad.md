# Thai Tofu Salad (Thai Beef Salad with tofu)

RecipeTin Eats' Thai beef salad as the user makes it, with seared tofu instead of steak and soy sauce instead of fish sauce: herby lettuce, tomato, cucumber and peanuts in a sharp lime, soy and chili dressing. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])

## Ingredients
**Dressing**
- ½ tsp [[thai-chili|bird's eye chili]], deseeded and finely minced
- ¼ tsp finely minced [[garlic]] (½ small clove)
- 1 tbsp finely chopped [[cilantro]] stems
- 2¼ tsp white [[sugar]]
- 2 tbsp [[soy-sauce]] (the original uses 2 tbsp fish sauce)
- 3 tbsp [[lime]] juice
- 1 tbsp [[neutral-oil]] (grapeseed, canola or vegetable)
- 1 small pinch [[kosher-salt]]

**Tofu**
- 8 oz [[extra-firm-tofu]], pressed (the original uses 7-8 oz / 200-250 g sirloin at room temperature)
- 1 tbsp [[neutral-oil]], ¼ tsp each [[kosher-salt]] and [[black-pepper]]

**Salad**
- 2 heaped cups [[mixed-greens|mixed lettuce leaves]]
- 10 [[cherry-tomatoes]], halved
- ¼ small [[red-onion]], very finely sliced
- ½ [[cucumber]], sliced
- ¼ cup each [[cilantro]] and [[mint]] leaves, lightly packed, plus extra to garnish
- ¼ cup finely chopped roasted, unsalted [[peanuts]]

## Method
1. **Dressing:** finely mince the chili, garlic and cilantro stems with a pinch of salt, then smear to a paste with the side of a [[chefs-knife]] (the source pounds them in a [[mortar-and-pestle]]; the user doesn't own one). Shake in a jar with the rest of the dressing ingredients, then adjust sugar, lime and soy sauce to taste.
2. **Tofu:** pat the pressed tofu very dry and cut into ~2 cm slabs. Heat a [[skillet]] over high heat until smoking ([[searing]]). Oil and season the tofu, then sear until deeply browned on both sides, a few minutes per side. Rest briefly and slice into strips. *(The tofu step is the user's adaptation; the source sears steak 2 min/side for medium-rare, rests it 10 min and slices against the grain.)*
3. **Salad:** toss the lettuce with 1 tbsp dressing. Toss the tofu with the other salad ingredients and most of the remaining dressing.
4. Pile the lettuce on plates, top with the tofu salad, sprinkle with peanuts and extra herbs, and drizzle with the last of the dressing. Serve immediately.

## Notes
- The dressing's sweet-sour-salty-hot balance is the whole dish. Measure garlic and chili by the teaspoon because sizes vary. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])
- The ground cilantro stem in the dressing is what gives it a "restaurant" flavor. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])
- Fish sauce is swapped for [[soy-sauce]] (user preference, 2026-11-14), which makes it vegan. Soy is less funky and a little darker; add a touch more lime if it tastes flat. Vegan fish sauce (seaweed or mushroom based) is closer in flavor if you find it.
- For beef as in the original: 7-8 oz sirloin, 2 min/side in a smoking skillet for medium-rare (52°C/125°F) or 2½ min for medium (57°C/135°F) on a 2 cm steak; rest 10 min tented.
