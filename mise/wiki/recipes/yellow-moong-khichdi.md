# Yellow Moong Khichdi

One-pot rice and yellow moong dal with a full tadka, cooked on the Instant Pot's Rice program. The comfort food of the collection.

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup [[yellow-moong-dal]], washed with the rice and drained
- 3/4 cup [[long-grain-white-rice]], washed with the dal and drained
- 1 tbsp [[neutral-oil]]
- 1 tbsp [[ghee]], or use 2 tbsp oil in total
- 1 tsp [[black-mustard-seeds]]
- 1 tsp [[cumin-seeds]]
- 1 sprig [[curry-leaves]]
- 1 tsp [[kasoori-methi]]
- 3-4 [[green-chili]], cut in half lengthwise
- 1 [[dried-red-chili]], split in two over the pot so the seeds fall in
- 5 clove [[garlic]], finely chopped
- 1 [[onion]], small, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp [[ground-cumin]]
- 1/2 tsp [[ground-coriander]]
- 1/4 tsp [[ground-turmeric]]
- 1/4 tsp [[asafoetida]]
- [[kosher-salt]], to taste
- 3/4 cup [[frozen-peas]] (optional)
- 1 [[carrot]], diced (optional)
- 1 cup [[spinach]] (optional)

## Method
1. Wash the rice and moong dal together, drain, and set aside.
2. Finely chop the onion and garlic, halve the green chilies lengthwise, and split the dried red chili in two over the pot so its seeds fall in.
3. Set the [[instant-pot]] to sauté and heat 1 tbsp oil with 1 tbsp ghee (or 2 tbsp oil).
4. Add, in this order: mustard seeds; cumin seeds once the mustard sputters; curry leaves and kasoori methi; all the chilies; garlic; onion; then the drained rice and dal. Mix well ([[blooming-spices]]).
5. Add the ground spices — cumin, coriander, turmeric and asafoetida — and mix.
6. Add water at 1 part rice-and-dal to 2 parts water, plus about 3/4 cup more for a mushier khichdi. Add the peas, carrot or spinach if using. Salt the water and taste it.
7. Turn off sauté, seal the lid, and run the Rice program ([[pressure-cooking]]).

## Notes
- **The water ratio is the reusable part:** 1 part rice and dal to 2 parts water, plus about 3/4 cup extra if you want it mushier.
- **Taste the water for salt before sealing the pot** — the source calls this out, and it is the last chance to adjust.
- Tadka order matters: the mustard seeds must sputter before the cumin goes in, or the cumin burns while the mustard is still raw.
- Splitting the dried red chili over the pot keeps its seeds in the dish, which is where most of its heat is.
- Ghee is optional — the source offers "2 tbs oil" instead, which makes the dish vegan.
- The source says only "Rice" with no variety; [[long-grain-white-rice]] is the wiki's generic page, but basmati is the likely intent for a khichdi.
- Oil, ghee, salt and water appear only in the method, never in the source's ingredient list.
- Both times are estimates — the source gives none, and does not say which release to use after the Rice program.

### Scaling
- Scales linearly; keep the 1:2 water ratio.
- Don't fill an Instant Pot past the half mark with grains and pulses — they foam.
