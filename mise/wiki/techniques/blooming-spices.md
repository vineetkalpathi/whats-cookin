# Blooming Spices

Briefly frying spices in hot fat. Many aroma compounds are fat-soluble, so blooming pulls them into the oil, which then carries them through the whole dish. In Indian cooking it's called *tadka* or *chhaunk*.

## How to
1. Heat the fat until it shimmers.
2. Add **whole** spices first: they take 20–40 seconds and are done when they crackle, pop, or darken one shade.
3. **Ground** spices take 10–20 seconds. Turn the heat down, or add them along with something wet (onions, tomato) so they don't scorch.

## Doneness cues
Strong aroma, a slight darkening, and seeds popping. Smoke or black specks mean it's burnt. Start over, because the bitterness carries into the whole dish.

## Common mistakes
- Ground spices in oil that's too hot. They burn in seconds.
- Crowding the pan with too little fat, so the spices toast unevenly.
