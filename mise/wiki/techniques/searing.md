# Searing

Cooking a surface fast in a very hot pan to brown it (Maillard reaction) while the inside stays tender. Browning needs a dry surface and heat well above boiling, so moisture is the enemy.

## How to
1. Heat the [[skillet]] over high heat until "screaming hot and smoking". (per [[thai-beef-salad]])
2. Oil the food rather than the pan, and season it.
3. Lay it in and don't move it until it releases and is browned, then flip.
4. Meat: rest, tented loosely with foil, about 10 min, then slice against the grain. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])

## Doneness cues
- Steak, 2 cm thick: 2 min/side to medium-rare (52°C/125°F internal), 2½ min/side to medium (57°C/135°F). (per [[thai-beef-salad]])
- Tofu: deep golden crust that releases from the pan on its own.

## Common mistakes
- Wet food (pat tofu and meat dry), a pan that isn't hot enough, or crowding it: all of these steam instead of searing.
