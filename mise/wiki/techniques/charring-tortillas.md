# Charring Tortillas

Heating [[corn-tortillas]] on high heat makes them pliable, toasts the corn flavor, and adds char spots.

## How to
1. Heat a dry [[skillet]] (or comal) until very hot, or use a gas burner flame directly.
2. Cook 30–45 seconds per side until puffed and spotted.
3. Stack them in a towel or tortilla warmer so they steam and stay soft.

## Common mistakes
- Not hot enough: the tortillas dry out and crack instead of puffing.
