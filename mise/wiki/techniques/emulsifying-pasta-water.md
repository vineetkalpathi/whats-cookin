# Emulsifying with Pasta Water

The starch dissolved in pasta water stabilizes a fat-and-water emulsion, so oil or butter and cheese become a glossy sauce that coats the noodles instead of pooling.

## How to
1. Cook the pasta in less water than usual so it's starchier, and pull it 1–2 minutes early.
2. Finish cooking it in the pan with the fat and a ladle of pasta water, tossing vigorously over high heat.
3. Adjust: add water if it's tight or greasy, and keep reducing if it's soupy.

## Doneness cues
The sauce is opaque and glossy, with no oil slick on the bottom of the pan.

## Common mistakes
- Adding cheese over high heat, so it clumps. Take the pan off the heat first.
