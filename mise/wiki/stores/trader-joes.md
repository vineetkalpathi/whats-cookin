# Trader Joe's

## General patterns
*Seeded from general knowledge. Confirm on trips and record what you find below.*
- Small, mostly private-label range, with fewer SKUs than a regular supermarket. Specialty and international spices are limited to a handful of jars and blends.
- Unlikely to carry: South Asian specialty items (asafoetida, curry leaves, amchur, whole spices in bulk), most dried Mexican chiles beyond a few, and specialty cuts of meat.
- Fresh herbs are sold in clamshells or small bunches, and produce is often pre-packed by count rather than loose.
- Good for: frozen items, cheese, nuts, snacks, basic produce, and pantry staples.
