# Middle Eastern

The cooking of the Levant, the Gulf, Turkey, and Iran. Shares its olive oil base and vegetable-forward habits with [[mediterranean]], but leans more on warm spices, sesame (tahini), pulses, yogurt, and sour notes from lemon, pomegranate molasses, and sumac.

## Flavor principles
- **Warm spice over hot spice.** Cumin, coriander, cinnamon and allspice rather than chile heat.
- **Sour as a seasoning:** lemon, sumac, pomegranate molasses, dried lime.
- **Sesame:** tahini in sauces and dips, sesame seeds on bread.
- **Bread as a utensil.** Many dishes are built to be scooped, which shapes their texture.
