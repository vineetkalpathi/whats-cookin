# Thai

Built on balancing salty, sour, sweet and hot in every dish, with lots of fresh herbs. Only one recipe so far, so this page is thin; it'll grow with curries, stir-fries and noodle dishes.

## Flavor principles
- **The dressing balance (yam):** salty [[fish-sauce]] + sour [[lime]] + sweet sugar + hot [[thai-chili]]. Taste and adjust each before dressing. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])
- **Pounded aromatics:** chili, garlic and cilantro stem pounded to a paste in a mortar, the start of dressings and curry pastes. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])
- **Fresh herb finish:** handfuls of [[cilantro]] and [[mint]] (and Thai basil), plus [[peanuts]] for crunch.

## Spice profile
| Spice | Form | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| [[thai-chili]] | fresh, minced or pounded | raw in dressings; measure by the teaspoon |
| garlic, cilantro stem | pounded | the paste base of dressings |

Common blends: curry pastes (red, green, massaman). *No pages yet.*

## Common ingredients
- **Aromatics:** garlic, shallot, lemongrass, galangal, makrut lime leaf
- **Produce:** cucumber, cherry tomato, red onion, lettuce
- **Proteins:** beef, chicken, shrimp, tofu
- **Starches:** jasmine rice, rice noodles
- **Fats & acids:** neutral oil, coconut milk, [[lime]]

## Pantry starter kit
Fish sauce, palm or white sugar, limes, Thai chilis (they freeze), jasmine rice, a curry paste, coconut milk.
