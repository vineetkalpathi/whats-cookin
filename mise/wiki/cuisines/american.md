# American

This page is a catch-all, and worth being honest about: it covers the Western home-cooking register that several of Amma's recipes are written in — American food-blog salads, a blended cream-of-vegetable soup, a tea-time cake. It is not a claim about American cuisine as a whole, which is far more regional and interesting than anything filed here so far.

What the recipes on this page actually share is a *method* register rather than a flavour one: build a bowl from raw vegetables and a grain, shake a dressing in a jar, and season to taste rather than to a measure. Four of the five give no quantities at all.

Several of them are also quietly fusion. [[asparagus-soup]] opens with a tadka — garlic, then green chili, then onion — before turning into a flour-thickened blended soup. [[asian-cabbage-salad]] is an American pan-Asian slaw rather than anything from one Asian kitchen. That mixing is the real character of this collection, and it belongs here rather than being forced onto [[indian]] or [[thai]].

## Flavor principles
- **Acid and fat as the dressing, assembled cold.** Olive oil with lemon or lime, sometimes bound with [[mayonnaise]] or honey mustard, shaken rather than emulsified by hand.
- **Raw vegetables carry the dish**, with a grain ([[quinoa]], pasta) as ballast rather than as the centre.
- **Sweet-and-salty accents:** dried cranberries with feta, corn with lime, toasted nuts over everything.
- **Season to taste, not to a measure.** The recipes here are templates; the cook decides.

## Spice profile
There isn't one, and that is the point — the flavour comes from acid, dairy and fresh herbs rather than from a spice base. Where spice does appear it arrives as a pre-mixed blend (an all-purpose seasoning, a chili-lime blend) rather than as individual spices bloomed in fat.

## Common ingredients
- **Grains:** [[quinoa]], [[pasta]]
- **Produce:** [[butternut-squash]], [[asparagus]], [[celery]], cabbage, cucumber, avocado, sweet corn
- **Dressings:** [[mayonnaise]], [[honey-mustard]], [[olive-oil]], [[lemon]]
- **Dairy:** [[feta]], [[sour-cream]], [[milk]], [[butter]]

## Pantry starter kit
Olive oil, lemons, quinoa, a jar of mayonnaise and one of honey mustard, a carton of vegetable broth, and whatever vegetables look good. Nothing here needs a special trip.
