# Russian & Ukrainian

The cooking of Russia and Ukraine, represented here by one deeply-specified family recipe. The page is named for both countries deliberately: [[borscht]] is as Ukrainian as it is Russian, and the source that brought it into this wiki simply calls it "Russian Soup".

The character of this food is the opposite of the [[indian]] pages. There is almost no spice box — a bay leaf, a lot of black pepper, maybe some chili flakes — and the depth comes instead from slow-cooked aromatics, sweet root vegetables, a sharp acid at the end, and dairy stirred in at the table.

## Flavor principles
- **Caramelised onion as the base.** Cooked past translucent to slightly brown ([[caramelizing-onions]]), often with tomato paste, until the fat separates. This is where the depth comes from, not from spice.
- **Sweet roots, sharp finish.** Beets, carrot and cabbage are sweet; [[red-wine-vinegar]] goes in at the very end to cut them. Without the acid the soup tastes flat and cloying.
- **Dairy at the table, not in the pot.** A dollop of [[sour-cream]] per bowl, stirred in by the eater.
- **Dill in quantity**, and used twice — half cooked into the pot, the rest raw on top.
- **Bread is part of the dish**, not an accompaniment: dark [[rye-bread]], toasted and buttered.

## Spice profile
| Spice | Form | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| [[bay-leaf]] | whole leaf | into the fat with the onion and garlic; the source calls it by its Indian name, *tej patta* |
| [[black-pepper]] | freshly ground, coarse | added generously at the end with the vinegar, not at the start |
| [[red-pepper-flakes]] | dried flakes | optional heat, added during the sauté rather than at the table |

That is close to the whole list. Caraway, allspice and horseradish belong to the wider cuisine but have no pages here yet.

## Common ingredients
- **Roots and vegetables:** [[beet]], [[carrot]], [[green-cabbage]], [[russet-potato]], [[onion]], [[celery]]
- **Dairy:** [[sour-cream]], [[butter]]
- **Acid:** [[red-wine-vinegar]]
- **Herbs:** [[dill]]
- **Bread:** [[rye-bread]], pumpernickel, black bread

## Pantry starter kit
Beets, cabbage, carrots, potatoes, onions, a jar of tomato paste, red wine vinegar, bay leaves, sour cream, a bunch of dill, and a dark rye loaf.
