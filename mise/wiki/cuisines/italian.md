# Italian

Strongly regional and ingredient-first: few components, high quality, handled simply. Spice is used sparingly. Herbs, cheese, cured pork, and good oil carry the flavor.

## Flavor principles
- **Soffritto**: onion, carrot, and celery sweated slowly in olive oil or butter, the base of ragù and soups.
- **Aglio, olio e peperoncino**: [[garlic]] gently infused in [[olive-oil]] with chile. See [[pasta-aglio-e-olio]].
- **Pasta water as an ingredient**: starch turns fat into a sauce. See [[emulsifying-pasta-water]].

## Common ingredients
- **Aromatics:** [[garlic]], onion, celery, carrot
- **Herbs:** [[parsley]], basil, rosemary, sage
- **Dairy:** [[parmesan]], pecorino, mozzarella
- **Starches:** dried pasta ([[spaghetti]]), risotto rice, bread
- **Fats & acids:** [[olive-oil]], butter, lemon, wine

## Pantry starter kit
Good olive oil, dried pasta, canned whole tomatoes, a wedge of parmesan, garlic, red pepper flakes, and anchovies.
