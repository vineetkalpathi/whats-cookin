# Mediterranean

A loose umbrella rather than one national cuisine: the cooking of the countries around the Mediterranean, from Spain and Italy through the Balkans and the Levant to North Africa. What they share is olive oil as the primary fat, a lot of vegetables, bread or grains at the centre, and a preference for fresh herbs and acid over long-cooked richness.

Overlaps heavily with [[middle-eastern]] and [[italian]]; recipes often belong to several at once.

## Flavor principles
- **Olive oil as flavour, not just fat.** Dishes are built in it and often finished with more of it raw.
- **Soffritto-style base:** onion, pepper, and garlic softened in oil before anything else goes in.
- **Fresh herbs at the end.** Parsley and mint go on off the heat, so they stay bright.
- **Acid to finish:** lemon, vinegar, or a pickle, rather than sweetness.
