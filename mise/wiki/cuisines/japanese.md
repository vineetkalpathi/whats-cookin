# Japanese

Umami-driven and restrained with spice: flavor comes from soy, dashi, miso, mirin and toasted sesame rather than dried spices. The only recipe so far ([[creamy-udon-noodles]]) is a fusion bowl that borrows Chinese chili crisp, so treat this page as a stub.

## Flavor principles
- **Soy-mirin-dashi:** the base of noodle broths and simmered dishes.
- **Garnish finish:** [[scallion]], toasted [[sesame-seeds]], shichimi togarashi.

## Spice profile
| Spice | Form | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| shichimi togarashi | blend | sprinkled on noodles and rice bowls |

## Common ingredients
- **Aromatics:** [[scallion]], ginger, garlic
- **Starches:** [[udon-noodles]], soba, short-grain rice
- **Proteins:** tofu, eggs, fish
- **Seasonings:** [[soy-sauce]], miso, mirin, rice vinegar, sesame oil

## Pantry starter kit
Soy sauce, mirin, rice vinegar, miso, frozen udon, sesame seeds, dashi (instant).
