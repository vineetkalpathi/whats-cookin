# Mexican

Built on corn (nixtamalized masa), beans, and chiles, with strong regional differences: Oaxacan moles, Yucatecan achiote, northern flour tortillas.

## Flavor principles
- **Dried chiles**: toasted, rehydrated, and blended into sauces (ancho, guajillo, chipotle, pasilla).
- **Charred aromatics**: tomatoes, onions, garlic, and chiles blistered on a comal before blending into salsas.
- **Fresh finish**: raw white [[onion]], [[cilantro]], and [[lime]] over nearly everything.

## Common ingredients
- **Starches:** [[corn-tortillas]], masa, rice
- **Legumes:** [[black-beans]], pinto beans
- **Produce:** tomatoes, tomatillos, white onion, fresh chiles, avocado
- **Dairy:** [[queso-fresco]], cotija, crema

## Pantry starter kit
Corn tortillas, canned black beans, dried ancho and guajillo chiles, Mexican oregano, cumin, limes, white onions, and cilantro.
