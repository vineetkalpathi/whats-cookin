# Mortar and Pestle

For pounding aromatics (chili, garlic, herb stems, salt) into a paste. Pounding crushes cells rather than slicing them, so it releases more flavor than chopping. A pinch of salt acts as an abrasive. (per [[thai-beef-salad]])

*The user doesn't own one (2026-11-14). Use the knife method under Substitutes.*

## Use & care
- Granite is the all-rounder for pastes; rinse with water only, no soap soaking.

## Substitutes
- [[chefs-knife]]: mince finely, then smear to a paste with the side of the knife on the board.
