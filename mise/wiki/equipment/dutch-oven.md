# Dutch Oven

A heavy, lidded pot (usually enameled cast iron). It holds steady heat for browning, braising, curries, soups, and bread.

## Use & care
- Preheat gradually. Enamel can crack under thermal shock.
- Soak off stuck fond with warm water and a wooden spatula.
