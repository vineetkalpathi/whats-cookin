# Chef's Knife

The all-purpose 8–10" blade. A sharp knife is safer than a dull one because it doesn't slip.

## Use & care
- Hone it often and sharpen it a few times a year. Hand wash only.
- Cut on wood or plastic boards. Glass and stone boards dull the edge.
