# Skillet

A 10–12" flared pan (stainless, cast iron, or carbon steel). A dry cast-iron skillet also works as a comal for tortillas.

## Use & care
- Stainless: preheat before adding oil to cut down on sticking.
- Cast iron and carbon steel: dry on heat, then wipe with a thin coat of oil.
