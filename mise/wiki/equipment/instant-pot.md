# Instant Pot

Electric multicooker: sauté, pressure cook, slow cook. The user cooks several recipes in one (owned per the recipe notes).

## Use & care
- **Sauté → pressure in one pot:** on high sauté, bloom spices and soften aromatics, then add liquid and **scrape the bottom clean** before sealing, or it may throw a burn warning. (per [[instant-pot-chana-masala-punjabi-chole]])
- Press Cancel before switching from sauté to pressure cook.
- A glass lid on sauté mode speeds up softening onions.
- Wash the sealing ring separately; it holds onto curry smells. A spare ring for sweet dishes helps.

## Substitutes
- Stovetop pressure cooker: same times at high pressure.
