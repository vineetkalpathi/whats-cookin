# Amma's recipes: Yellow Moong Khichidi

Two-page illustrated Instant Pot khichdi: equal parts rice and yellow moong dal, a full staged tadka, and a 1:2 water ratio. Ends at the Rice program with no serving line.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- Equal parts rice and yellow moong dal, 3/4 cup each, feeds 2-3.
- **Water ratio:** 1 part rice-and-dal to 2 parts water, plus about 3/4 cup extra for a mushier khichdi. The most reusable thing on the page.
- Tadka order is explicit: mustard seeds, then cumin once they sputter, then curry leaves and kasoori methi, then chilies, then garlic, then onion, then the grains.
- Ground spices go in *after* the grains, not into the hot fat.
- Salt the water and taste it before sealing the pot.
- Ghee is swappable for oil, which makes the dish vegan.

## Capture flags
