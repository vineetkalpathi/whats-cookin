# Amma's recipes: Methi Lagaan (Jyoti)

Two-page baked savoury cake of rava, besan and maida loaded with methi, carrot, corn and onion, bound with equal parts milk and oil. Page 2 is process photos.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- The oven-baked cousin of handvo: a rava-and-besan batter heavy with greens, baked at 350°F for 35-40 minutes.
- **Equal parts milk and oil, 1/2 cup each** — unusually oil-rich, and what keeps it moist.
- Vegetables are mixed with the salt and rava *before* the flours go in.
- Both leaveners go in last, right before the pan.
- Sesame seeds are roasted and split: half into the batter, half as a top crust.
- Methi and palak are given as equal alternatives, fresh or frozen.
- Served hot, cut into squares, with chutney or ketchup.

## Capture flags
Attributed to Jyoti. Salt is "1 tsp (Swad Anusar)" — to taste. The source prints "2/3 pods of garlic", read as 2-3 cloves, and says only "chili powder" without specifying the type.
