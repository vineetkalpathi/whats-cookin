# Thai Beef Salad (RecipeTin Eats)

Seared sirloin sliced thin and tossed with lettuce, cherry tomato, red onion, cucumber, cilantro, mint and peanuts in a lime / fish sauce / chili dressing. Serves 2, 25 minutes. The user makes it with tofu instead of beef: see [[thai-tofu-salad]].

## Key takeaways
- The dish lives or dies on the **balance of the dressing**: salty (fish sauce), sour (lime), sweet (sugar), hot (chili). Adjust to taste before dressing.
- Measure garlic and chili **by the teaspoon**, not by the clove or chili, because sizes vary.
- Pound chili, garlic, cilantro **stems** and a pinch of salt into a paste in a [[mortar-and-pestle]]; the ground cilantro stem adds a "restaurant" dimension. No mortar: mince, smear to a paste with the side of a knife, and shake in a jar with the rest.
- Sear in a **screaming hot, smoking** skillet: 2 min/side for a 2 cm steak to medium-rare (52°C/125°F), then rest 10 min tented and slice against the grain.
- Dress the lettuce lightly (1 tbsp) separately from the other salad ingredients, then pile up and serve immediately.
