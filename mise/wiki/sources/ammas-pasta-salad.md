# Amma's recipes: Pasta Salad

A mayo-and-lemon pasta salad with an opinionated storage rule: the mayonnaise and onion go in only at serving. No quantities, and the time header was left blank.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A two-stage salad: an olive-oil-and-lemon base, with mayonnaise and raw onion folded in only at serving.
- **The storage warning is the point** — mayo and onion added early and stored overnight go "watery and gross".
- Carrot or broccoli gets a 2-minute blanch only, left with bite.
- Basil is dressed in with the oil and lemon, not scattered at the end.
- No quantities and no times: a template, not a measured recipe.

## Capture flags
The "Servings | Prep Time | Total Time" header is an unfilled template. No quantities for any ingredient, including the pasta. Carrot and broccoli read as either/or. Pasta shape unspecified.
