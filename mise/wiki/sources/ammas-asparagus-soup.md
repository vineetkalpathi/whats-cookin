# Amma's recipes: Asparagus Soup

A one-page numbered method for a blended asparagus soup, with a tadka-style aromatic start and a teaspoon of flour as a light thickener. No ingredient list as such — the quantities are inside the steps.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A Western blended cream-of-vegetable soup with an Indian aromatic start.
- **Garlic first and only 2-3 seconds**, then green chili, then onion.
- A 1 tsp all-purpose-flour step, cooked about a minute before any liquid.
- Liquid is an explicit either/or: 2 cups vegetable broth, or 2 cups water plus a big tablespoon of bouillon paste.
- Blended, then finished with milk off the heat.
- Works in a pan or in the Instant Pot on sauté.

## Capture flags
No asparagus quantity, no milk quantity, no yield and no timings anywhere. "2-3 spoons of oil" is ambiguous. Step 1 says to discard the soft tips as well as the hard ends, which is unusual — kept as written rather than silently corrected.
