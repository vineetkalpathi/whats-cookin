# Amma's recipes: Tomato-Mozzarella Panini

A one-page sandwich build with no quantities at all: tomato, fresh mozzarella and jalapeños on ciabatta, pressed. Photographed served with tomato soup.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- **Butter on the outside, green chutney or pesto on the inside** — the source is specific about which goes where.
- Green chutney in place of pesto is the household twist on a caprese panini.
- Jalapeños, preferably pickled, bring the heat.
- Salt, pepper and chili flakes season the filling directly, not the bread.
- Bread is open: ciabatta, torta rolls, or any thick bread sturdy enough to press.
- No quantities and no timings — a build, not a measured recipe.

## Capture flags
"Green Chutney/Pesto" is one bullet covering two different products. "Mozzarella Cheese" is unqualified, though the photo shows fresh mozzarella. "Torta" means the Mexican roll, not a cake.
