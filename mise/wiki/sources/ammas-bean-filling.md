# Amma's recipes: Bean Filling

Two-page illustrated recipe (Google Docs export, with photos) for a make-ahead bean and vegetable
filling, explicitly intended for quesadillas, enchiladas, or "Mexican pasta". One of the
`ammas-recipes` PDFs. Shares its structure and sauteing method with
[[ammas-pasta-gravy]] - the same vegetable base, finished differently.

The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the raw file was made from page
renders.

## Key takeaways
- **It's a component, not a dish.** The output is stored in the fridge and deployed into three
  different meals, which is why it's written without servings or timings.
- **Chop everything to bean size.** The whole point is that one spoonful carries beans and
  vegetables together.
- **Order of the saute:** oil, then garlic, 3-5 seconds, then onion; vegetables once the onion is
  translucent; beans and pickled jalapenos last.
- **Jalapeno jar vinegar is an ingredient**, not a leftover: 2-3 spoons go in with the pickled
  jalapenos. It's the acid in the dish.
- **Texture is controlled by heat and the lid:** high heat and uncovered to evaporate fast for a
  crunchy bite; lower heat, or covered, to retain water and soften the vegetables. This is stated
  as the explicit lever to pull.
- **Spices go in at the end**, after the vegetables are cooked - roasted cumin, black pepper,
  crushed red pepper, optional taco seasoning - with cilantro stirred through off the heat.
- **Vegetables are a template**, not a fixed list: "other veggies of your choice, like..."
