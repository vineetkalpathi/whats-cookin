# Amma's recipes: Borcht – Russian Soup (Kumar)

Beet and cabbage soup written as a bulleted ingredient list plus one prose paragraph of method. Page 2 is photographs only. The author's stated point of difference is caramelizing the onions.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- **Caramelizing the onions is the author's stated difference** — "the one different thing".
- Second cue: cook the onion and tomato paste until the mixture "leaves oil".
- Vegetables are staged — potato, carrot and celery first; cabbage, spring onion, dill and beets once the potato is about 30% done.
- Finished with red wine vinegar and crushed black pepper, boiled a minute or two.
- Sour cream and fresh dill go on at the table, never into the pot.
- Vegetable or meat broth is an explicit choice; heat comes from crushed red pepper added during the sauté.
- Served with toasted, buttered black or pumpernickel bread.

## Capture flags
Attributed to Kumar and titled "Russian Soup". Vinegar is 3-4 tbsp in the list but "2/3 tbs" in the method. The fresh tomato is listed but never explicitly added. The method's final sentence has no terminal punctuation and page 2 is photos only, so a trailing clause may have been lost. Bay leaf is called "tej patta".
