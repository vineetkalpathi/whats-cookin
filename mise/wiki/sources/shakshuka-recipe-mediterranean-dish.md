# Shakshuka Recipe (The Mediterranean Dish)

Eggs poached in a simmered sauce of tomatoes, green bell peppers, onion and garlic, seasoned with
coriander, sweet paprika and cumin, finished with parsley and mint. One skillet, up to 6 servings,
about 40 minutes. The site tags it Mediterranean / Middle Eastern / Tunisian, breakfast or lunch,
vegetarian and gluten-free.

Captured with Obsidian Web Clipper after the site returned HTTP 403 to both `capture.py` and a
direct web fetch.

## Key takeaways
- **Build the sauce before the eggs.** Soften onion, pepper, garlic and the ground spices together
  (~5 min), then add tomatoes and tomato sauce, cover and simmer 15 minutes, then uncover and
  reduce until thick. A watery sauce won't hold the wells.
- **Fresh tomatoes, not canned:** 6 medium tomatoes, chopped (about 6 cups) plus 1/2 cup tomato
  sauce for body.
- **Wells, spaced out.** Make 6 indentations with a wooden spoon and crack one egg into each, so
  they set as distinct eggs rather than scrambling into the sauce.
- **Cover to finish the eggs** on medium-low, just until the whites set. The residual heat keeps
  cooking them after the pan leaves the stove.
- **Herbs go on after the heat is off:** parsley and mint, raw.
- **Make-ahead:** the sauce keeps overnight in the fridge; reheat and start again from the egg
  step. Leftovers (with the eggs) only keep 1-2 days.
- **Meat variation:** brown 1/2 lb ground beef or lamb separately, wipe the skillet, then add it
  back to simmer with the tomatoes before the eggs go in.
