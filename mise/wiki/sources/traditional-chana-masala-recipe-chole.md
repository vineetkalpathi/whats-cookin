# Traditional Chana Masala Recipe (Chole) (Ministry of Curry)

Restaurant-style Punjabi chole: soaked dried chickpeas pressure-cooked (optionally with a black tea bag), then simmered in a stovetop onion-tomato masala seasoned with a chana masala spice blend and finished with kasoori methi. Serves 4. Total time 9 hours with the overnight soak; about 1 hour of cooking.

## Key takeaways
- Soak dried chickpeas 8-10 hours, then pressure cook 35 minutes with natural release.
- A **black tea bag** in the cooking water gives chole its dark color and a "rich smoky" flavor; discard it after cooking.
- Brown the onions with a **bay leaf** in ghee to light golden (8-10 min); a lid speeds it up.
- Spicing comes mainly from a store-bought **chana masala spice blend** (1-2 tbsp), plus turmeric and Kashmiri chili powder.
- Use the chickpea cooking liquid in the curry, adding water to reach the consistency you want.
- Finish with **kasoori methi** (dried fenugreek leaves) and cilantro.
