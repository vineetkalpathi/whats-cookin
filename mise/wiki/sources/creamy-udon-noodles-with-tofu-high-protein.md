# Creamy Udon Noodles with Tofu (Dr. Vegan)

Vegan, high-protein noodle bowl using tofu two ways: silken tofu blended with soy, rice vinegar, sugar and chili crisp into a creamy sauce, and extra-firm tofu crumbled, browned and glazed (soy, dark soy, tomato paste, nutritional yeast, smoked paprika). Serves 2, 35 minutes. The site labels it "Asian inspired".

## Key takeaways
- **Silken tofu as a cream base:** blend until completely smooth; warm it gently, never at a hard boil.
- **Browning tofu crumbles:** crumble into small, uneven pieces, spread out in a wide nonstick skillet over medium-high and leave it between stirs, 10-15 min, until dry with golden patches.
- Glaze over medium heat 3-5 minutes until reduced and coating; pull it off before it burns (sugar + tomato paste).
- Use precooked (frozen or vacuum-packed) udon; reserve ½ cup cooking water and **don't rinse** for a warm dish. Add the water a tablespoon at a time only if needed.
- Taste the noodles together with the topping before adjusting seasoning.
