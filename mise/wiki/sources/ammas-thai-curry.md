# Amma's recipes: Thai Curry

A one-page family template for vegetable and tofu curry from jarred Thai paste. **Quantities are given for only three ingredients** — everything else is a bare name.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- An improvisational template rather than a measured recipe: a vegetable list, an aromatic order, and a paste-plus-coconut finish.
- **Aromatic order is explicit** — green chili, then garlic and ginger, then onion, then mint, "put in that order".
- The aromatics are fried until they look "cooked/shiny" before the paste and a little water go in.
- Hard vegetables get par-cooked separately only when the volume is high.
- Tofu simmers covered in the gravy *before* the coconut goes in, so it takes on flavour.
- Coconut milk and coconut cream both go in at the end and cook only briefly.

## Capture flags
No servings, no times, and no quantities for 18 of 21 ingredients. Oil and red chili powder are used in the method but missing from the list. "Thai kitchen paste" is probably the Thai Kitchen brand, which is shrimp-free, but the wiki does not assume that.
