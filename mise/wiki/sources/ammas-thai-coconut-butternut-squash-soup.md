# Amma's recipes: Thai Coconut Curry Butternut Squash Soup

A fully measured blog-style recipe — the most precisely specified source in the collection — giving both a stovetop and a slow cooker route to the same blended soup.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- Two routes, one finish: a 45-minute stovetop simmer or 3-8 hours in a slow cooker.
- **Red curry paste is fried with the aromatics for 3 minutes** before any liquid — that is where the flavour is built.
- Coconut milk and lime go in *after* blending and *off* the heat, never boiled.
- Part of the coconut milk is held back purely for the swirled garnish.
- Dry-roasted peanuts and cilantro are treated as part of the dish, not decoration.
- Heat is pushed to the table via sriracha rather than built into the base.

## Capture flags
Offers chicken or vegetable broth, so the diet tag depends on the choice. Step 3 hedges the lime as "(if using)" though it is listed unconditionally.
