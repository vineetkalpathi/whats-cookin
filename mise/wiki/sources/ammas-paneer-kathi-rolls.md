# Amma's recipes: Paneer Kathi Rolls

Two-page recipe for 6 servings: paneer in a yogurt-and-spice marinade, a fast pepper-and-onion filling, and store-bought paratha as the wrapper.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A yogurt marinade carries the entire masala: Kashmiri chili, garam masala, turmeric, roasted cumin, coriander, amchur, kasoori methi, green chili, ginger, garlic and salt.
- **Amchur is the sourness**, working alongside the yogurt.
- Sauté the green chili, garlic and ginger briefly before they go into the marinade.
- The filling is fast on purpose: onions and peppers 3-4 minutes, then the paneer 3-4 minutes more.
- Frozen paratha (the source names Kawan) is the wrapper; 6-8 for 6 servings.
- Assembly: chutney on the paratha, filling down the middle, raw onion and cilantro, then roll. Both green and sweet chutney.

## Capture flags
Garlic is used in the filling step but is absent from the ingredient list and has no quantity. No marinating time is given, and the chutneys have no recipes or quantities.
