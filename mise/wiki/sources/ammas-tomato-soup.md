# Amma's recipes: Tomato Soup

Tomato soup thickened with dry-roasted yellow moong dal rather than cream, made in batch for bottling. The one recipe in the set that states a yield.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- **The dal is the body** — no cream, no roux.
- Dry roasting the dal for 2-3 minutes is the step the source singles out, and it says why: "to get nice aroma".
- **Rinse the dal after roasting, not before.**
- Tomatoes go in only when the dal is three-quarters soft.
- Liquid is managed by topping up to keep everything submerged, then thinned to taste at the end.
- Cool before blending — stated explicitly.
- Optional finish of ghee and roasted cumin; made as "3 bottles worth" and refrigerated.

## Capture flags
Yield is stated as 3 bottles, but the photo shows four filled jars.
