# Instant Pot Chana Masala (Punjabi Chole) (Ministry of Curry)

One-pot Instant Pot chole: a cumin-seed tadka in ghee, onions, ginger-garlic and ground spices, then the soaked chickpeas pressure-cooked right in the masala. Tomatoes and garam masala go in after cooking. Serves 4. Total 5 h 10 min with a 4-hour soak, about 1 hour hands-off cooking.

## Key takeaways
- Soak dried chickpeas in warm water at least 4 hours (or overnight).
- Bloom cumin seeds in hot ghee on high sauté until they sizzle, about 1 minute; cover the onions with a glass lid to speed softening.
- Deglaze: stir in the water and scrape loose browned bits before sealing (prevents the burn warning).
- Pressure cook 35 min (soaked dried) or **5 min (canned)** with natural release. For canned chickpeas, use only ½ cup water.
- Tomatoes and garam masala go in **after** pressure cooking, sautéed 5 minutes, so the tomato stays fresh.
- Serve with paratha or puri, or jeera / basmati rice for gluten-free.
