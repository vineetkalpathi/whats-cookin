# Amma's recipes: Killu Bonda and Chutney

A single page carrying two recipes — deep-fried vegetable bondas on a four-flour batter, and a dal-tempered onion-tomato chutney to go with them. **Ingredients only: the page gives no method for either.**

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A four-flour batter — wheat, all-purpose, rice and besan — loaded with shredded vegetables and deep-fried.
- Idli batter is the preferred binder and leavener, with a plain-flour fallback given explicitly.
- **Rice flour is what makes them crisp** rather than bready.
- Aromatics are the standard South Indian set: ginger, green chili, hing, cilantro and curry leaves.
- Baking soda or eno salt gives the lift.
- The chutney's structure comes from fried urad and chana dal, with tamarind for sourness.
- "Killu" refers to pinching off the portions by hand.
