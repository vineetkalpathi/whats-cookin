# Amma's recipes: Almond Flour Cake

A flourless almond sponge leavened with whipped egg whites, with chocolate chips layered through the middle. The worst scan in the collection — a screenshot with solid black bands, and no title on the page itself.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A flourless almond-flour sponge leavened by whipped whites, baked 30 minutes at 350°F in an 8-inch round pan.
- **The sugar splits three ways:** 2 tbsp to coat the buttered pan, 1/4 cup into the yolks, 1/4 cup into the whites.
- The batter is a thick dough before the whites go in; folding in 1/2-cup additions is what makes it smooth and fluffy.
- **Chocolate chips go as a layer in the middle**, between two halves of batter.
- Cool 5 minutes in the pan, loosen with a knife, invert onto a plate.
- Naturally gluten-free via almond flour — but the baking powder and chocolate chips need a label check.
