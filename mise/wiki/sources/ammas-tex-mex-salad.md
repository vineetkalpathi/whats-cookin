# Amma's recipes: Tex-Mex Salad

A fully quantified quinoa, black bean and corn salad with a whisked lime-and-spice marinade. One of the few sources in the set that measures its ingredients.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A Tex-Mex quinoa bowl on 1 cup dry quinoa (about 3 cups cooked) plus roughly equal cups of corn, black beans and red cabbage.
- The dressing is a whisked spice marinade carried by lime juice, not a vinaigrette — **the oil is explicitly optional**.
- Three blends do the flavouring: coriander-cumin (dhana jeera), chili-lime, and an all-purpose seasoning.
- **Avocado goes in only at serving**, so everything else can be mixed ahead.
- Serves warm or chilled, which makes it a good potluck dish.

## Capture flags
No servings and no times. The list gives 1.5 cups water to 1 cup quinoa but step 1 says to follow the packet, usually 2:1. The asterisk after "Tri-Colored Quinoa" has no matching footnote.
