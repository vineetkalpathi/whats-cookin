# Amma's recipes: Pasta Gravy

Two-page illustrated recipe (Google Docs export, with photos) for a sauteed-vegetable pasta sauce.
Titled "Pasta Gravy" in the original; filed in the wiki as [[veggie-pasta]] at the user's request,
to match the name they use. One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs, and the sibling of
[[ammas-bean-filling]] - the same vegetable base and the same saute method, finished with jarred
pasta sauce instead of beans and Mexican spices.

The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the raw file was made from page
renders.

## Key takeaways
- **It's a template, not a fixed recipe:** the vegetables are "of your choice", and the sauce is
  "pasta sauce of your choice (red, pesto, etc.)". The constant is the method.
- **Chop to bite size** here, rather than the bean size used in [[bean-filling]].
- **Order of the saute:** oil, then garlic, 3-5 seconds, then onion; vegetables once the onion is
  translucent; cook to the bite you want.
- **Texture is controlled by heat and the lid**, exactly as in [[bean-filling]]: high and uncovered
  for crunch, lower or covered for soft.
- **Season at the end** - black pepper, crushed red pepper, salt - and finish with chopped basil
  off the heat.
- **Jarred sauce is assumed.** No tomatoes are cooked down; the vegetables are the dish and the
  sauce is a dressing for them.
