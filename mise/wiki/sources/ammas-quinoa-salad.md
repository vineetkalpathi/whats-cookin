# Amma's recipes: Qinoa Salad

A chopped vegetable salad where quinoa is a minor component, with a honey-mustard dressing shaken in a bottle. Almost nothing is measured, and the time header was left blank.

One of the `ammas-recipes` PDFs. The PDF has no extractable text layer; the transcription in the
raw file was made from page renders at ingest.

## Key takeaways
- A fridge-clearing chopped salad — quinoa is about 1/2 cup cooked, not the base.
- **Quantities are deliberately unspecified**: a template, not a measured recipe.
- The dressing is shaken in a bottle: olive oil and lemon juice roughly 4:1, plus honey mustard, cumin, garlic powder, salt, pepper and chili flakes.
- Dried cranberries and feta are the sweet-salty hook.
- Carrot may be raw-grated or lightly cooked; parsley and dill are interchangeable.

## Capture flags
The "Servings | Prep Time | Total Time" header is an unfilled template — the three words with no values. No quantities for 11 of 12 salad ingredients. The source spells quinoa "QINOA" throughout.
