# Tomato Sauce

Plain, unseasoned cooked and strained tomato - the can labelled "tomato sauce" in US stores, close to Italian passata. Not the same thing as a jarred pasta sauce, which is seasoned ([[pasta-sauce]]).

Used in small amounts for body and colour: it thickens without adding the water that fresh tomatoes do.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]], [[mediterranean]]

## Choosing & storing
Check the label for added salt, sugar, or herbs. Once open it keeps about 5 days in the fridge, or freeze it in an ice cube tray.

## Substitutes
- [[crushed-tomatoes]]: 1:1, chunkier; blend or simmer longer for a smooth result
- [[tomato-paste]]: 1 tbsp paste + 3 tbsp water per 1/4 cup sauce, more concentrated and sweeter
- 2 pureed fresh tomatoes per 1/4 cup, per [[instant-pot-authentic-mexican-rice]]
