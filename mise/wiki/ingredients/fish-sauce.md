# Fish Sauce

Fermented anchovy and salt liquid. The salty, savory pillar of Thai dressings, balanced against lime, sugar and chili. (per [[thai-beef-salad]]) Keeps for a long time; refrigerate after opening.

**User note (2026-11-14):** the user swaps fish sauce for [[soy-sauce]] (e.g. in [[thai-tofu-salad]]).

**Cuisines:** [[thai]]

## Substitutes
- [[soy-sauce]]: 1:1, vegan, loses the funk; add a squeeze of lime
- vegan fish sauce: 1:1, closest flavor (seaweed or mushroom based)
