# Feta

Brined white cheese, traditionally sheep's or sheep-and-goat's milk: salty, sharply tangy and
crumbly, slightly chalky. It seasons a dish as much as it enriches it. In [[quinoa-salad]] it is
the salty half of the sweet-salty hook, working against the [[dried-cranberries]].

## Choosing & storing
A block sitting in brine beats a tub of pre-crumbled by a wide margin — crumbled feta is drier,
often dusted with an anti-caking starch, and tastes duller. Greek sheep's-milk feta is the
tangiest; cow's-milk versions are milder and firmer. Keep the block in its brine and it holds for
weeks; out of brine it dries and hardens within days, so cover a brineless piece in lightly salted
water.

## Prep notes
Crumble it by hand over the bowl at the end. It shatters into shards rather than cubes, and it does
not melt. Taste before salting anything it goes into.
