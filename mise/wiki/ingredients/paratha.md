# Paratha

Layered unleavened flatbread: fat is folded through the dough so it cooks up flaky and comes apart
in sheets, where [[naan]] is leavened, soft and chewy. Bought frozen here, by the pack —
[[paneer-kathi-rolls]] uses 6-8 of them, cooked to the packet instructions and used as the wrap,
and the source names Kawan without insisting on it.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
The frozen aisle of an [[indian-grocery]]. Plain is the one for a roll; malabar and stuffed
versions are their own dish. Keep them frozen and separate the discs while they are still
frozen — once thawed they fuse into a single mass that is hard to rescue, and they do not refreeze
well.

## Prep notes
Cook from frozen in a dry, properly hot skillet; no oil is needed. It puffs and the layers separate
when it is ready, and pressing the puff down with a cloth helps them open. Keep the cooked ones
stacked under a towel so they stay pliable — a cooled paratha cracks when you try to roll it.

## Substitutes
- [[naan]]: one per paratha as a wrap, softer and less flaky, and it tears more easily when rolled
- flour tortilla: 1:1, rolls very well, none of the flakiness (no page yet)
