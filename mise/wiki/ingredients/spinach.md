# Spinach

A soft green leaf, mild and faintly mineral, that wilts to almost nothing: a large bag collapses to a couple of spoonfuls. Optional in [[yellow-moong-khichdi]], stirred through at the end for colour and a bit of body. It is also the stated alternative to fresh methi in [[methi-lagaan]], but it is a much plainer leaf: methi's bitterness is the character of that dish, so with spinach the result is milder and sweeter rather than the same thing.

## Choosing & storing
Baby spinach is the convenient one, already washed and stemmed. Bunched mature spinach has more flavour and stands up to cooking, but the bunches hold grit, so wash in a bowl of water rather than under the tap and pull the thick stems off. Either way it keeps only three to five days and wilts to slime fast. Store it dry, loosely wrapped, with a sheet of kitchen paper in the bag, and never wet. Frozen chopped spinach is good in cooked dishes if squeezed out hard first.

## Prep notes
- Add it in the last minute or two of cooking. Any longer and it goes army green and loses its texture entirely.
- It releases a lot of water. If the dish needs to stay thick, wilt it separately and squeeze it before stirring in.

## Substitutes
- [[fenugreek-leaves]]: 1:1 by volume, far more bitter and aromatic, a different dish rather than a swap
