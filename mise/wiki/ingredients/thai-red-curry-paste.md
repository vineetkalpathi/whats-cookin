# Thai Red Curry Paste

A pounded paste of dried red chillies, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallot, coriander root and usually shrimp paste. Deeply savoury and aromatic as much as hot, and the backbone of both [[thai-curry]] and [[thai-coconut-butternut-squash-soup]]. In both it is fried in oil, or in the thick part of the coconut milk, before any liquid goes in; unfried it tastes raw and sharp instead of rounded. **Most brands contain shrimp paste or fish sauce, and that is the reason neither of those recipes is tagged vegan.** A few brands are genuinely vegan and say so clearly on the tub, so it is worth reading the label if that matters. Green paste is an equal alternative in both recipes: same quantity, more herbal, often hotter.

**Cuisines:** [[thai]]

## Choosing & storing
Thai brands in tubs or small tins (Maesri, Mae Ploy and similar) are much stronger and better than most supermarket jars, and cheaper per spoonful. Heat varies enough between brands that a new tub deserves half the stated amount, tasted, then topped up. Unopened it keeps a year or more in the cupboard. Once open it keeps a couple of months refrigerated but steadily dulls and loses its top aromatics, so for a tub you use slowly, freeze it in tablespoon blobs instead.
