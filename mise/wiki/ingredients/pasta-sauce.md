# Pasta Sauce

Seasoned jarred sauce, ready to use. Distinct from plain [[tomato-sauce]], which is unseasoned and used as an ingredient rather than a sauce.

In [[veggie-pasta]] the jar is explicitly a choice - red, pesto, or anything else - because the vegetables are the dish and the sauce dresses them.

**Cuisines:** [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Read the label for sugar. Pesto and many creamy or "alla vodka" red sauces contain cheese, so a jar can quietly make a vegan dish vegetarian. Refrigerate after opening and use within about a week.

## Substitutes
- [[crushed-tomatoes]]: a 28 oz can per jar, plus salt, garlic and herbs - you are making the sauce rather than buying it
