# Extra-Firm Tofu

Pressed block tofu, usually in the refrigerated case near produce. Holds its shape for searing and crumbling.

## Prep notes
- Pat dry (or press) before cooking; moisture is what stops it browning.
- **Crumbles:** crumble into small, uneven pieces, spread out in a wide nonstick pan over medium-high and leave it between stirs, 10-15 min, until dry with golden patches. (per [[creamy-udon-noodles-with-tofu-high-protein]])
- **Slabs:** sear in a very hot pan; see [[searing]].

## Substitutes
- [[silken-tofu]]: not interchangeable, it won't hold shape
