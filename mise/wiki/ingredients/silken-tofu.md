# Silken Tofu

Unpressed, custard-like tofu, sold refrigerated or in shelf-stable aseptic boxes. Blends completely smooth into a dairy-free cream sauce; warm it gently and don't hard-boil it. (per [[creamy-udon-noodles-with-tofu-high-protein]])

## Substitutes
- [[extra-firm-tofu]]: not interchangeable for sauces, it won't blend smooth
