# Ginger

Fresh rhizome. Scrape the skin off with a spoon and grate it on a microplane. It freezes well whole and grates straight from frozen. "1 inch" is roughly 1 tbsp grated.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Substitutes
- ground ginger: 1/4 tsp per 1 tbsp fresh, much less bright
