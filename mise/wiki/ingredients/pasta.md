# Pasta

Short dried pasta, the shape left open. [[veggie-pasta]] is a template recipe and the shape is one of the things that varies, so this page stands for whatever is in the cupboard.

For a chunky vegetable sauce, shapes with ridges or hollows (penne rigate, fusilli, rigatoni) hold onto it far better than smooth or long shapes. Where a recipe depends on a specific shape, it gets its own page - see [[spaghetti]].

**Cuisines:** [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Bronze-die pasta has a rougher, paler surface that sauce clings to. Keeps indefinitely sealed.

## Prep notes
Salt the water heavily. Cook to just under the box time if it will sit in a hot pan of sauce afterwards.

## Substitutes
- [[spaghetti]]: 1:1 by weight, but long strands suit smooth sauces more than chunky vegetable ones
