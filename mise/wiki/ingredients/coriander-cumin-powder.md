# Coriander-Cumin Powder

Dhana jeera: a pre-mixed blend of ground coriander seed and ground cumin, usually around two parts
coriander to one of cumin. Warm and earthy from the cumin, with the citrusy, slightly sweet edge
coriander brings — an everyday background spice rather than a feature. Buying it as a blend saves
reaching for [[ground-coriander]] and [[ground-cumin]] separately; [[tex-mex-salad]] uses 1 tsp of
it in the lime marinade.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
Sold in bags or tubs at an [[indian-grocery]]. Blends vary in ratio and in grind, so taste a new bag
before trusting a measurement. Keep it airtight and away from the stove, and replace it within a
year — ground spice fades fast, and the cumin goes dusty and flat first.

## Substitutes
- [[ground-coriander]]: with [[ground-cumin]], 2 parts coriander to 1 part cumin, same total volume; close enough that the blend is a convenience rather than a necessity
