# Zucchini

Mild summer squash, mostly water. That water is the thing to manage: it will either steam the pan or cook off and let the vegetables brown, depending on your heat and whether there's a lid on ([[saute-to-bite]]).

**Cuisines:** [[italian]], [[mediterranean]]

## Choosing & storing
Small and firm beats large - big zucchini go seedy and watery. Keeps about a week in the crisper.

## Prep notes
Add it later than the carrot and broccoli; it softens quickly and turns to mush if overcooked.
