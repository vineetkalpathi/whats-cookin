# Chocolate Chips

Chips are formulated to hold their shape: less cocoa butter than a bar, often with added stabilisers, so they soften in the oven without really flowing. That is why [[almond-flour-cake]] layers them through the middle of the batter instead of stirring them through. They stay as a distinct seam rather than sinking through a wet, gluten-free mixture to the bottom of the tin.

## Choosing & storing
Semi-sweet or dark around 50 to 60% suits most baking; milk chips make the cake cloying. If the ingredients list vegetable fat rather than cocoa butter, they will taste waxy. Keep them cool, dry and sealed. Warmth causes bloom, the dull grey streaking that is just fat risen to the surface and harmless, while the fridge makes them sweat and pick up smells. Sealed in the cupboard they keep a year or more.

## Prep notes
Do not melt chips for a glaze or ganache, since resisting melting is the point of them; chop a bar instead. When layering, keep the chips in from the edge of the tin so they do not weld the cake to the side.

## Substitutes
- chopped dark chocolate bar: 1:1 by weight, melts into pockets and streaks rather than holding its shape (no page yet)
