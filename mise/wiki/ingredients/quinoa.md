# Quinoa

A small seed cooked and eaten like a grain. Mild and faintly grassy, with a nutty pop from the little germ ring that uncoils as it cooks. It cooks in about 15 minutes with no soaking, and 1 cup dry gives roughly 3 cups cooked, which is the quantity both [[tex-mex-salad]] and [[quinoa-salad]] are built around. Served cold in both, where it holds dressing better than rice does.

**Cuisines:** [[american]]

## Choosing & storing
White quinoa is the mildest and softest; red and black hold their shape better in a salad but take a few minutes longer. Most boxed quinoa is pre-rinsed and says so on the packet. If yours does not, rinse it in a fine sieve until the water runs clear, otherwise the saponin coating on the seeds tastes soapy and bitter. Airtight in the cupboard it keeps about a year, but it carries some oil, so a musty or crayon-like smell means it has gone off.

## Prep notes
- 1 part quinoa to 1¾ parts water, simmer covered 15 minutes, then rest off the heat 10 minutes before forking through. Lifting the lid early leaves it wet.
- For salads, spread the cooked quinoa on a tray to cool. Dressing it hot in a bowl steams it into mush.
