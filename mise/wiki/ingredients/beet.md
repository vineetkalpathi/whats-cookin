# Beet

Dense, sweet root with a distinct earthy note that people either like or avoid. [[borscht]] shreds them raw rather than boiling them whole, and puts them in late on purpose: the red-violet pigment turns brown-orange with long cooking, so the colour of the finished soup is a matter of timing.

**Cuisines:** [[russian-ukrainian]]

## Choosing & storing
Small to medium ones with smooth skin are the sweetest; large beets go woody at the core and show pale rings when cut. Leaves still attached mean a recent harvest, and the tops cook like chard, but trim them off before storing or they draw moisture out of the root. Loose in a bag in the fridge they keep for weeks. Soft, wrinkled skin means it has been sitting a long time and the flavour has gone flat.

## Prep notes
Scrub rather than peel if the skin is thin, or peel after cooking when it slips off. They stain hands, boards and anything wooden, so gloves and a plastic board, or expect pink fingers for a day. Shred on the coarse side of a box grater. Adding the acid in a dish along with the beets helps hold the colour.
