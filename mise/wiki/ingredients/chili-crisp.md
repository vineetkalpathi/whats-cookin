# Chili Crisp

Chinese condiment of chili flakes, fried shallot/garlic and often soybeans in oil (Lao Gan Ma, Fly By Jing). Check labels if it needs to be vegan; some contain shrimp or MSG-heavy bouillon.

## Substitutes
- [[red-pepper-flakes]]: a pinch bloomed in oil, no crunch
