# Bell Pepper

One page for all colours, because they sit in the same place in the shop and swap freely in these recipes. The colour is ripeness, not variety: green is picked unripe and tastes grassy and slightly bitter; red, yellow, and orange are riper, sweeter, and softer when cooked.

Where a recipe cares, it says so - [[shakshuka]] wants green for that grassy edge, while the family recipes ([[bean-filling]], [[veggie-pasta]]) just say "colored peppers" and mean whichever looks good.

**Cuisines:** [[mediterranean]], [[middle-eastern]], [[mexican]]

## Choosing & storing
Firm, glossy, heavy, with a green stem. They keep 1-2 weeks in the crisper. Red and friends cost more because they stay on the plant longer.

## Prep notes
Cut around the core rather than through it. Chop to match the dish - bean size for [[bean-filling]], bite size for [[veggie-pasta]].
