# Sriracha

Smooth chili-garlic sauce with sugar and vinegar in it. Moderate heat, and the garlic and sweetness do as much work as the chili.

**Cuisines:** [[thai]]

## Choosing & storing
Keeps at room temperature unopened; the fridge after opening keeps the colour red rather than brown.

## Prep notes
Added to taste at the end of [[bean-filling]], with the other seasonings, rather than cooked in - the vinegar and sugar are worth keeping fresh.

## Substitutes
- [[chili-crisp]]: not 1:1, crunchy and oily rather than smooth, and no vinegar tang
