# Mayonnaise

An emulsion of oil, egg yolk and acid: rich, mild and faintly tangy, and what makes a dressed
salad creamy rather than oily. In [[pasta-salad]] it is folded in only at serving, with the raw
onion, because the source is blunt about what happens otherwise — left overnight, the salt pulls
water out of the vegetables and the whole thing goes watery.

**Cuisines:** [[american]]

## Choosing & storing
Full-fat behaves best; light versions are thinner and sweeter. Vegan mayonnaise, made with
aquafaba instead of egg, works much the same way. Refrigerate after opening and go by the date. It
is what blocks a vegan tag on [[pasta-salad]].

## Prep notes
Fold it through rather than stirring hard, and keep it off the heat — it thins when it is worked
and splits when it is warmed.

## Substitutes
- [[plain-yogurt]]: 1:1, tangier and thinner, and it weeps faster still
- [[sour-cream]]: 1:1, closer in richness, still thinner than mayonnaise
- vegan mayonnaise: 1:1, no real difference in a cold salad (no page yet)
