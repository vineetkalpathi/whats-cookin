# Kasoori Methi

Dried fenugreek leaves: savory, slightly bitter, maple-like aroma. Crush between your palms and stir in at the end, as in [[chana-masala]]. (per [[traditional-chana-masala-recipe-chole]]) A little goes a long way; store airtight.
