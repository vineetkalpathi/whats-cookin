# Garlic

The more finely garlic is cut, the sharper it gets: slices stay sweet, a microplaned paste is fierce. It burns fast in hot oil and turns bitter, so start sliced garlic in cool oil for gentle cooking (as in [[pasta-aglio-e-olio]]). A medium head has about 10 cloves.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]], [[italian]], [[mexican]]

## Substitutes
- [[garlic-powder]]: 1/8 tsp per clove, for long-cooked dishes only
