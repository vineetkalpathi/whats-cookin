# Ground Cumin

Pre-ground cumin. It's convenient, but it goes stale within months, so buy small jars. It burns in seconds in hot oil: bloom it briefly over lower heat, or add it along with something wet (see [[blooming-spices]]). In Mexican and Tex-Mex cooking it's used with restraint. In Indian cooking it's often toasted and ground fresh from [[cumin-seeds]].

**Cuisines:** [[indian]], [[mexican]]

## Substitutes
- [[cumin-seeds]]: about 1:1 by volume, toasted and ground. Fresher and stronger
