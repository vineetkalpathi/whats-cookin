# Tomato

The everyday round slicing tomato. Six medium ones give roughly 6 cups chopped. In cooked sauces they bring acidity and a lot of water, so they need time to reduce.

**Cuisines:** [[mediterranean]], [[middle-eastern]], [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Heavy for their size, fragrant at the stem. Never refrigerate - cold kills the aroma and turns the flesh mealy. Out of season, canned is usually better than a pale fresh one.

## Prep notes
For a sauce that has to hold its shape (like the wells in [[shakshuka]]), chop and then cook uncovered long enough to drive the water off.

## Substitutes
- [[roma-tomato]]: 1:1 by weight, meatier and less watery, better when the sauce must thicken
- [[crushed-tomatoes]]: a 28 oz can per 6 medium tomatoes, off-season insurance, less fresh
