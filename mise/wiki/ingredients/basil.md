# Basil

Sweet, faintly anise-scented soft herb. Heat destroys it, so it belongs at the end of cooking or off the heat entirely.

Thai basil is a different plant with a stronger anise note and sturdier leaves - not a swap for this.

**Cuisines:** [[italian]], [[mediterranean]]

## Choosing & storing
Bright green, no black edges. **Don't refrigerate** - basil blackens below about 4°C. Keep the stems in a glass of water on the counter.

## Prep notes
Tear rather than chop; a knife bruises the leaves and they darken. Stir it in off the heat, as [[veggie-pasta]] does.

## Substitutes
- [[parsley]]: 1:1, fresh and clean but none of the sweet anise note
