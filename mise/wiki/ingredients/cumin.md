# Cumin

Warm, earthy, slightly bitter seed. Whole seeds get bloomed in hot fat until they darken a shade and crackle (see [[blooming-spices]]). Ground cumin is best toasted briefly in the oil. Ground cumin goes stale within months.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]], [[mexican]]

## Substitutes
- caraway: 1/2 the amount, related but more anise-like (no page yet)
