# Vegetable Broth

Ready-to-use savoury liquid in a carton, used in place of water so a soup tastes of something from the start. Mild and vegetal, and brands differ enormously: the cheap ones are mostly salt, onion and yeast extract. The wiki keeps this separate from [[vegetable-bouillon-paste]] deliberately, because they are two different things to buy even though recipes list them as alternatives. Used in [[thai-coconut-butternut-squash-soup]], [[asparagus-soup]] and [[borscht]], and since it already carries salt, all three salt late and lightly.

## Choosing & storing
Taste a sip straight from the carton. If you would not drink it, boiling it will not improve it. Low-sodium versions give you control over seasoning and are much safer in a soup you intend to reduce. An unopened carton keeps a year in the cupboard; once open, refrigerate and use within four or five days, because it sours quietly without looking any different. If a recipe only wants two cups, freeze the rest in a jar or an ice-cube tray.

## Substitutes
- [[vegetable-bouillon-paste]]: 1 tsp in 1 cup hot water per cup of broth, usually saltier, so hold back on salt
- water plus a pinch of salt: 1:1, thinner and plainer but perfectly workable in a well-seasoned soup (no page yet)
