# Dark Soy Sauce

Thicker, aged, molasses-sweet soy sauce used mostly for color and a deep glaze. Less salty than regular [[soy-sauce]]. Mostly at Asian groceries.

## Substitutes
- [[soy-sauce]]: 1:1 plus a pinch of sugar or molasses; less color
