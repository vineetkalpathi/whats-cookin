# Dried Cranberries

Cranberries dried and sweetened — tart underneath, sweet enough to read as candy, chewy like a
raisin. In savoury cooking they do one job well: a sweet-sour punctuation against something salty.
With the crumbled [[feta]] they are the sweet-salty hook that lifts [[quinoa-salad]] out of
plain-salad territory.

## Choosing & storing
They should feel soft and pliable through the bag; hard ones that rattle have dried too far.
Almost all are sweetened with sugar or juice and given a light oil coating. Unsweetened versions
exist and are aggressively sour. Sealed in the cupboard they keep for months, and if they do stiffen,
a few minutes in warm water plumps them back.

## Substitutes
- raisins: 1:1, sweeter and without the tartness that makes the salad work (no page yet)
- chopped dried apricot: 1:1, softer, more jammy than sharp (no page yet)
