# Kashmiri Chili Powder

Ground mild red chili prized for deep red color with gentle heat. Not the same as "chili powder" (the Tex-Mex blend) or cayenne, which is much hotter.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Substitutes
- paprika: 1:1 plus a pinch of cayenne, for color with mild heat (no page yet)
- [[smoked-paprika]]: 1:1, adds smoke
