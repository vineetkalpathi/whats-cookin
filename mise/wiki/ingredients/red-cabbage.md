# Red Cabbage

The same vegetable as [[green-cabbage]] with anthocyanin pigment in the leaves. A little firmer, coarser and more peppery, and it holds its crunch longer once dressed, which is why it is the one used raw and shredded thin in [[asian-cabbage-salad]] and [[tex-mex-salad]]. The colour bleeds: acid in a dressing turns it bright pink, so a dressed salad looks livelier after an hour and tints anything pale sitting next to it.

## Choosing & storing
Pick a head that feels heavy for its size with tight, squeaky outer leaves and a cut stem that is still pale rather than dried and split. Whole in the fridge drawer it keeps for weeks. Once cut, wrap the cut face and use it within a week; the exposed surface greys and the smell turns cabbagey. Half a head goes a long way in a salad.

## Prep notes
- Quarter it, cut the wedge of core out of each quarter, then slice across the leaves as thin as you can manage. Thick shreds are chewy and refuse to take dressing.
- Do not salt it for these salads. Salting draws water out and softens it, which is the opposite of what a raw slaw wants.

## Substitutes
- [[green-cabbage]]: 1:1, milder and paler, and it does not bleed colour
