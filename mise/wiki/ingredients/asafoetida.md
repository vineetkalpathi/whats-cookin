# Asafoetida

Dried resin of a giant fennel, sold ground (usually cut with flour). Pungent raw, but a pinch bloomed in hot fat goes savory and allium-like. A key part of many dals and tadkas. Store it airtight, because the smell travels.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Substitutes
- [[garlic]]: a little extra garlic + onion approximates the savory, allium note
