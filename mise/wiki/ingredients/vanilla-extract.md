# Vanilla Extract

Vanilla beans extracted into alcohol. A teaspoon does not make something taste of vanilla so much as round it out, and that is its job in [[almond-flour-cake]], where it stops the almond and sugar tasting flat. Real extract is more complex than synthetic vanilla flavouring, but once a cake is baked the gap between them is small, so either works here.

## Choosing & storing
Look for "pure vanilla extract" with vanilla beans and alcohol on the label. Keep it capped tight in a dark cupboard, where it lasts years. The alcohol evaporates from a loose cap and leaves it weak and syrupy. Do not refrigerate it.

## Prep notes
- Add it with the wet ingredients and off the heat. The aroma is volatile and prolonged heat drives most of it off, which is why it is wasted in anything boiled for long.
