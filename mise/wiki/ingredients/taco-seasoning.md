# Taco Seasoning

Pre-mixed blend, typically chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic and onion powder, oregano, and a lot of salt. Tex-Mex rather than Mexican.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]]

## Choosing & storing
Blends vary widely in salt and heat, so taste before committing a full spoon. Many also contain cornstarch as a thickener, which matters if the dish is already dry.

## Prep notes
Optional in [[bean-filling]] (~1 tsp), added at the end with the other spices.

## Substitutes
- [[ground-cumin]] + [[smoked-paprika]] + [[garlic-powder]]: roughly 2:1:1, plus salt and oregano, close enough
