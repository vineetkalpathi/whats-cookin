# Udon Noodles

Thick, chewy Japanese wheat noodles. Precooked (frozen or vacuum-packed) udon only needs heating: loosen the strands, reserve some cooking water, and don't rinse for a warm dish. (per [[creamy-udon-noodles-with-tofu-high-protein]]) Frozen udon has the best chew.

**Cuisines:** [[japanese]]
