# Naan

Leavened flatbread, traditionally slapped onto the wall of a tandoor: soft, chewy and blistered,
and richer than a roti because the dough carries yogurt or milk. Softer and puffier than
[[paratha]], which is unleavened and flaky instead. Bought ready-made here, and used alongside a
dish rather than in it — [[thai-coconut-butternut-squash-soup]] offers it for dipping, which is a
household liberty rather than anything Thai.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
The refrigerated and frozen ones are much better than the shelf-stable packs, which are drier and
oddly sweet. It stales fast at room temperature, so freeze what is not being eaten and reheat it
straight from frozen.

## Prep notes
Reheat it dry and hot — a minute a side in a hot skillet, or a moment under a grill. A microwave
makes it tough. A brush of butter or [[ghee]] as it comes off the heat is the point of naan.

## Substitutes
- [[paratha]]: one per naan, flaky and fried rather than soft and chewy
- pita or any thick flatbread: 1:1 for dipping (no page yet)
