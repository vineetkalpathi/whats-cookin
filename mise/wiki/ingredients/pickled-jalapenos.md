# Pickled Jalapeños

Sliced jalapeños in vinegar brine. Milder than fresh - pickling knocks the heat back - and they bring sharp acidity along with it.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]]

## Choosing & storing
Jarred or canned; both keep for months in the fridge once opened.

## Prep notes
**Keep the jar vinegar.** In [[bean-filling]] 2-3 spoons of the brine go in with the jalapeños and act as the acid of the dish (per [[ammas-bean-filling]]). Treat the liquid as an ingredient, not packing.

## Substitutes
- [[green-chili]]: fresh, hotter and without the acid; add a splash of vinegar to compensate
