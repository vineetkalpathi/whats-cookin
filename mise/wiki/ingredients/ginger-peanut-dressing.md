# Ginger Peanut Dressing

A pourable peanut-and-ginger salad dressing: peanut butter, grated ginger, soy sauce and something
acidic, loosened with water or oil and sweetened a little. Nutty, sharp and salty, and thick enough
to cling to raw cabbage. It is the whole dressing of [[asian-cabbage-salad]], about 3/4 cup added a
little at a time, and it is normally made at home in a couple of minutes in a jar rather than
bought.

**The family's recipe was not captured.** The source page behind [[asian-cabbage-salad]] is an
excerpt, and the dressing's own recipe is missing from it. Nothing here reconstructs it. Until the
full post is re-captured — queued in `mise/inbox.md` — a bought bottle is the stand-in.

## Choosing & storing
Bottled versions sit with the salad dressings or the Asian sauces. They vary a lot in sweetness and
heat, so taste before pouring, and check the label for fish sauce or honey if that matters.
Refrigerate after opening. A homemade batch keeps about a week in the fridge and thickens as it
sits; loosen it with a spoonful of warm water and shake.

## Prep notes
Add it to cabbage a little at a time, tossing as you go. It is thick, and an over-dressed slaw is
hard to undo.
