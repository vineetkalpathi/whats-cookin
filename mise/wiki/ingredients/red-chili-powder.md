# Red Chili Powder

Plain ground hot red chilli, sold as lal mirch powder. Heat first, flavour second, with none of the cumin, oregano and garlic that American "chili powder" blends carry (see [[taco-seasoning]], which is a different thing entirely). **It is not [[kashmiri-chili-powder]]**, which is mild and used mostly for colour, so swapping one for the other either guts the heat or multiplies it. It gives [[methi-lagaan]] its bite, and in [[thai-curry]] it is the dial for adjusting heat after the paste is in.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
Brands vary enormously in heat, so with a new packet add half what the recipe says and taste. Keep it airtight, dark and away from the hob. It fades over about a year: bright red dulls to brown-red, the smell flattens and the heat goes soft, at which point you end up adding too much.

## Substitutes
- [[kashmiri-chili-powder]]: not interchangeable, roughly 3-4x the volume for the same heat and a much redder dish
- cayenne pepper: 1:1, near enough identical in use (no page yet)
