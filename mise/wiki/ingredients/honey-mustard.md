# Honey Mustard

Bottled mustard sweetened with honey: thick, mild and sweet-sharp, far gentler than plain mustard.
It makes a useful ready-made dressing base, and in [[quinoa-salad]] it goes into the shaken bottle
with olive oil and lemon juice, doing the emulsifying as well as the seasoning. It is also the one
ingredient that blocks a vegan tag on that salad.

## Choosing & storing
Brands differ mostly in how sweet they are, so add it to taste rather than by a measure. Check the
label if vegan matters — the honey is the obvious problem, and some versions also contain egg. It
keeps for months in the fridge once opened, and may separate, so shake it.

## Substitutes
- plain mustard plus a sweetener: about 2 parts mustard to 1 part honey or maple syrup, sharper; with maple it is vegan (no page yet)
