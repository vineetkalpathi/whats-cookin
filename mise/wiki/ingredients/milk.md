# Milk

Whole dairy milk. In both recipes here it is doing the same kind of work — enriching rather than
flavouring. [[methi-lagaan]] takes 1/2 cup, exactly matching the 1/2 cup of oil, and between them
they are what keep that baked batter moist. [[asparagus-soup]] takes a splash at the end to loosen
the blended soup and soften its edge.

## Choosing & storing
Whole milk unless a recipe says otherwise; low-fat will loosen a soup but not enrich it. Fridge, by
the date, at the back rather than in the door.

## Prep notes
Milk stirred into a soup at the end must not boil: it catches on the pan and can curdle against
anything acidic. Take the pan off the heat first.

## Substitutes
- unsweetened plain plant milk: 1:1; it makes [[asparagus-soup]] vegan, which that recipe notes outright (no page yet)
