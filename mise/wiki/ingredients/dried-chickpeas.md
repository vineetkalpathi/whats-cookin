# Dried Chickpeas

Dried chickpeas, bought by the bag. Cheaper than canned, with a firmer, nuttier result and a flavorful cooking liquid that becomes part of the curry. 1 cup dried makes roughly 2½-3 cups cooked.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Prep notes
- Soak 4 hours minimum in warm water, or 8-10 hours / overnight, then drain. (per [[instant-pot-chana-masala-punjabi-chole]], [[traditional-chana-masala-recipe-chole]])
- Soaked chickpeas pressure-cook in about 35 minutes with natural release. See [[pressure-cooking]].
- Old beans stay hard no matter how long you cook them; buy from a store with good turnover.
## Substitutes
- [[chickpeas]]: about 2 cans per 1 cup dried; skip the soak and cut pressure time to 5 min
