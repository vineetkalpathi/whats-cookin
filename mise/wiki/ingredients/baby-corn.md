# Baby Corn

Immature maize cobs eaten whole, cob and all. Nothing like [[sweet-corn]], which is mature kernels: baby corn is barely sweet and is there for crunch and shape. [[thai-curry]] uses the canned sort, which is already cooked and only needs warming through in the sauce.

**Cuisines:** [[thai]]

## Choosing & storing
Buy tins of whole spears in brine rather than cut pieces, and drain and rinse them, as the brine tastes tinny and slightly sour. If the spears are yellowed, collapsing or smell sharp out of the tin, that is the tin gone and no amount of rinsing fixes it. Leftovers keep three or four days in a tub of fresh water in the fridge. Fresh or frozen baby corn is noticeably crunchier if you can find it.

## Prep notes
Halve the fat spears lengthways or cut them on a slant so they cook evenly and are easier to eat. Add them towards the end of a curry; left to simmer they go soft and lose the one thing they were brought in for.

## Substitutes
- water chestnuts: 1:1, same crunch, no corn flavour (no page yet)
- [[sweet-corn]]: not a real swap, much sweeter and with no bite at all
