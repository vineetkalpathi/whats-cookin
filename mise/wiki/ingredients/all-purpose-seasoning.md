# All-Purpose Seasoning

A pre-mixed dried blend of alliums, herbs and pepper. The one meant here is Trader Joe's 21 Seasoning Salute, which is salt-free; most jars labelled "all-purpose seasoning" are mostly salt. That makes the quantity in a recipe unreliable across brands, so in [[tex-mex-salad]] treat it as a flavour and add [[kosher-salt]] separately to taste. Composition varies too (onion, garlic, celery seed, black pepper, citrus peel, mixed herbs in some ratio), so two jars will not taste the same.

## Choosing & storing
Read the label before you buy: if salt is the first ingredient, you are buying seasoned salt, and the amount a recipe calls for will be far too much. Ground blends fade faster than whole spices, so buy small jars; if the jar smells of nothing when you open it, it is only adding dust. Keep it away from the hob, as steam cakes the powder into a solid plug.
