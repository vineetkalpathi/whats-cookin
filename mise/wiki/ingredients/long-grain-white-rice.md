# Long Grain White Rice

The default rice for pilaf-style dishes where the grains should stay separate. Long grains hold less of the sticky amylopectin starch than medium or short grain, so they fluff rather than clump.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]]

## Choosing & storing
Keeps indefinitely in a sealed container. Nothing to choose between brands at this price point.

## Prep notes
**Rinse until the water runs clear, then drain well.** Surface starch is what makes pressure-cooked rice gluey. Draining matters as much as rinsing when the recipe toasts the rice in oil afterwards ([[toasting-rice]]) - wet rice steams instead of browning.

Under pressure, use about 1:1 rice to water, far less than stovetop, because nothing evaporates (per [[instant-pot-authentic-mexican-rice]]).

## Substitutes
- basmati: 1:1, more aromatic and even drier; reduce liquid slightly (no page yet)
- jasmine: 1:1, softer and stickier, less suited to a pilaf-style rice (no page yet)
