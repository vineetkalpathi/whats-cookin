# Edamame

Young green soybeans, sold shelled and frozen. Firm, faintly sweet and grassy — closer to a fresh
pea than a dried bean — and they keep their bite in a dressing instead of going soft. In
[[asian-cabbage-salad]] they carry the protein, about a cup and a half thawed among all that raw
cabbage.

## Choosing & storing
Buy them shelled (mukimame) for salads; the in-pod kind is a snack you would have to pod by hand.
Beans that move freely in the bag have stayed frozen, a solid clump has thawed and refrozen. They
keep for months in the freezer and are better bought frozen than fresh, which is rare and short-lived.

## Prep notes
For a salad they only need thawing: a few minutes in cold water, or a minute in boiling water then
drained and cooled. Cooked any further they go mealy and lose the point.

## Substitutes
- [[frozen-peas]]: 1:1, sweeter and softer, with much less protein
- [[chickpeas]]: 1:1, heavier and starchier, but the same job in a cold salad
