# Sour Cream

Cream soured with cultures, around 18 to 20% fat: thick, rich and gently tangy. Fatter and more mellow than [[plain-yogurt]], which is thinner and sharper. In [[borscht]] it goes on at the table, a dollop per bowl, and is never stirred into the pot. Two reasons: it splits at a simmer, and everyone wants a different amount. Stirred into a hot bowl it also tempers the sweet-sour of the beets and turns the soup pink.

**Cuisines:** [[russian-ukrainian]]

## Choosing & storing
Full fat, with cream and cultures and little else. The low-fat tubs are mostly starch and gums and break into grains the moment they meet hot soup. Keeps a couple of weeks unopened and about a week once open. A watery top layer is whey and stirs back in; mould or a fizzy, prickling sourness means it has gone.

## Substitutes
- [[plain-yogurt]]: 1:1, tangier and thinner, whole-milk or Greek is closest
- crème fraîche or smetana: 1:1, richer and far less likely to split (no page yet)
