# Thai Chili

Small, very hot fresh chili. Measure minced chili by the teaspoon rather than by the chili, since sizes vary. (per [[thai-beef-salad]]) Freezes well whole. More often at Asian groceries than regular supermarkets.

**Cuisines:** [[thai]]

## Substitutes
- serrano: about 2x the amount, less heat (no page yet)
