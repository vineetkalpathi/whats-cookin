# Pesto

Basil pounded with pine nuts, garlic, hard cheese and olive oil. Grassy and salty with a raw garlic bite, and unlike [[pasta-sauce]] it is never simmered: it goes in cold, as a spread or a finisher. It is the alternative to [[green-chutney]] in [[tomato-mozzarella-panini]], playing the same role of a sharp green layer under the cheese. Most jars contain parmesan or pecorino, so jarred pesto is not vegan unless the label says so.

**Cuisines:** [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Jars from the chiller cabinet taste fresher than the shelf-stable ones. Look for basil and olive oil near the front of the ingredients list rather than sunflower oil and potato flakes. Once open, keep it refrigerated and smooth the surface flat under a film of oil; the top darkens to khaki where it meets air, which is harmless but tastes tired. It freezes well in ice-cube trays.

## Substitutes
- [[green-chutney]]: 1:1 in the panini, herbier, spicier, no cheese
