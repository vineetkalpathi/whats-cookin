# Carrot

Sweetness and colour, and one of the few vegetables that holds its shape through long cooking. In the family vegetable recipes it is part of the standard set that goes in whatever else is around.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]], [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Firm, smooth, no cracks or white bloom. Cut the greens off before storing or they pull moisture from the root. They keep for weeks in the crisper.

## Prep notes
Carrot is the slowest-cooking vegetable in most mixed-vegetable sautes, so either cut it smaller than the rest or give it a head start.
