# Chana Masala Powder

A store-bought Punjabi spice blend made for chickpea curry: typically coriander, cumin, dried mango (amchur), pomegranate seed (anardana), chili, black salt, and warm spices. Tangier than garam masala. Brands (MDH, Everest, Shan) vary a lot. Use 1-2 tbsp per 4 servings and adjust to taste. (per [[traditional-chana-masala-recipe-chole]])

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Substitutes
- [[garam-masala]]: 1:1 plus a little amchur and extra coriander; less tangy
