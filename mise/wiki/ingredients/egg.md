# Egg

Large eggs are the default in recipes unless stated otherwise. When eggs are the centre of the dish rather than a binder, freshness shows: a fresh white holds together instead of spreading.

**Cuisines:** [[mediterranean]], [[middle-eastern]]

## Choosing & storing
Keep refrigerated in the carton. To test age, lower one into water - a fresh egg lies flat on the bottom, an old one stands up or floats.

## Prep notes
For [[poaching-eggs-in-sauce]], crack each egg into a cup first if you are not confident, then slide it into its well. Cook covered on low heat: the whites set from the sauce around them, and the yolks keep going after the pan leaves the heat.
