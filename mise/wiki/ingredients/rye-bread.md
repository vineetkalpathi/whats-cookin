# Rye Bread

A dense dark loaf, sour and earthy, covering everything from a seeded deli rye to black bread and pumpernickel. Rye carries little gluten, so the loaves are close-crumbed and heavy, and most supermarket "rye" is largely wheat with some rye flour for colour and tang. It is served alongside [[borscht]], toasted and buttered, not in it. Buttered rye against hot sweet-sour beet soup is half the point of the meal.

**Cuisines:** [[russian-ukrainian]]

## Choosing & storing
Buy the darkest, densest loaf available, ideally a proper sourdough rye from a bakery counter; caraway seeds in it are traditional and worth having. It stales within a day or two and the fridge makes that faster, not slower. Keep what you will eat wrapped at room temperature and freeze the rest sliced, then toast straight from frozen. Stale rye toasts perfectly well, so staling only rules out eating it raw.
