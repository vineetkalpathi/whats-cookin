# Olive Oil

Extra-virgin for flavor and finishing. In [[pasta-aglio-e-olio]] it's the sauce, so use a good one. Store away from light and heat, and use it within a few months of opening.

**Cuisines:** [[italian]]

## Substitutes
- [[neutral-oil]]: 1:1 for cooking, loses the fruity, peppery flavor
