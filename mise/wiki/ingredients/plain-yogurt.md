# Plain Yogurt

Whole-milk yogurt with nothing added, sold as dahi or curd. Thick, tangy and mildly sweet. In Indian cooking its job is to carry spice and tenderise at the same time: in the [[paneer-kathi-rolls]] marinade it holds the whole masala against the paneer, so the spices stay on the cubes instead of sliding into the pan. Not the same as [[sour-cream]], which is cultured cream, fatter, richer and less acidic.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
Buy whole-milk plain yogurt; the low-fat tubs are thickened with gums and taste chalky in a marinade. Greek yogurt is strained and much thicker, fine here if loosened with a spoon of water. Keeps a couple of weeks unopened and about a week once open. A pool of watery whey on top is normal, just stir it back in; sour fizz, pink spots or mould mean bin it.

## Prep notes
- Whisk it smooth before adding anything else, or the marinade goes lumpy.
- It splits if boiled. Add it off the heat or on a very low flame, never into a hot dry pan.

## Substitutes
- [[sour-cream]]: 1:1, richer and less tangy, splits more easily
