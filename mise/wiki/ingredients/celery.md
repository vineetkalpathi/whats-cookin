# Celery

Crunchy and saline with a bitter, faintly aniseed edge. It is a background flavour more than a vegetable in its own right: cooked, it is one leg of the standard soup base, and [[borscht]] shreds it along with the other vegetables so it disappears into the broth. Raw in [[quinoa-salad]] it does the opposite job and provides the bite.

## Choosing & storing
Buy a whole tight head rather than trimmed sticks. The stalks should snap cleanly and the leaves look perky, as the leaves are the first part to wilt and a good indicator of age. Bendy stalks and a hollow, spongy centre mean it has been in storage. Keep the head wrapped in the fridge; stalks that have gone slightly limp will crisp up again standing in cold water for an hour.

## Prep notes
The outer stalks are the stringy ones, so either peel them or keep them for stock and use the pale inner stalks raw. Slicing thin across the stalk cuts the strings short enough that nobody notices them. The leaves are worth chopping into a salad.

## Substitutes
- fennel: 1:1 raw, sweeter and openly aniseed (no page yet)
- celery seed: 1/4 tsp per stalk for cooked dishes, gives the flavour but none of the texture (no page yet)
