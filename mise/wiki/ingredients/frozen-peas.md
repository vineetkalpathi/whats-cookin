# Frozen Peas

One of the few frozen vegetables that beats fresh in practice - they're frozen within hours of picking, while "fresh" peas turn starchy in a day.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]], [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
Keep them in the freezer and feel the bag: a solid block means it thawed and refroze. Loose, rattling peas are what you want.

## Prep notes
They need almost no cooking. Fold them into something already hot and let residual heat do it - in [[instant-pot-mexican-rice]] they go in after the lid comes off. Boiling them turns them grey and dull.
