# Black Beans

Small, earthy beans common in Mexican, Central American, and Caribbean cooking. Canned (15 oz, about 1.5 cups drained) is the weeknight default. Dried beans cooked with onion and epazote have more flavor.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]]

## Substitutes
- [[chickpeas]]: 1:1, firmer and nuttier, holds shape more
