# Kosher Salt

Coarse salt that's easy to pinch. **Brands differ in density:** Diamond Crystal is about half as salty by volume as Morton, so recipes should say which they mean.


## Substitutes
- table salt: about half the volume (Diamond Crystal) or 3/4 (Morton), it's denser
