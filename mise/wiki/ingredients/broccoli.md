# Broccoli

Sold by the crown; one medium crown gives roughly 3-4 cups of small florets. Holds crunch well in a hot saute, which is what the family vegetable recipes are after.

**Cuisines:** [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Tight, deep green heads with no yellowing. Yellow means the buds are opening and it will taste sulphurous. Keeps about a week in the crisper, loosely wrapped.

## Prep notes
Don't throw out the stalk - peel the woody skin and it's sweeter than the florets. Cut the florets to the size the recipe calls for: bean size in [[bean-filling]], bite size in [[veggie-pasta]].
