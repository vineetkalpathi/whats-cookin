# Sweet Chutney

Thick, dark, sweet-sour chutney made from [[tamarind]] or dates with jaggery and warm spices, almost syrupy and glossy. It is the sweet counterweight to the heat of [[green-chutney]] in [[paneer-kathi-rolls]], and most people want a stripe of both rather than one or the other. Sold in jars at an [[indian-grocery]] under several names: sweet chutney, tamarind chutney, date and tamarind chutney, imli chutney, all much the same thing.

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Choosing & storing
Thick and very dark, with dates or tamarind near the front of the ingredients list rather than glucose syrup. Thin, pale jars taste mostly of sugar. All that sugar makes it keep for months in the fridge once open, but it stiffens in the cold, so loosen a spoonful with a little warm water before spreading. Wipe the jar rim; mould starts on the neck, not in the body.

## Substitutes
- [[tamarind]]: simmer the strained pulp with roughly an equal volume of jaggery or brown sugar plus a pinch each of ground cumin and chilli, about 10 minutes, for a rougher homemade version
