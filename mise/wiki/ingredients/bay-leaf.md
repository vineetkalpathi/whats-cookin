# Bay Leaf

Dried leaf fried whole in the fat at the start of a dish, removed before serving or left in. Note: Indian bay leaf (*tej patta*, cinnamon-tree family, cassia-like) and Mediterranean bay laurel are different plants; recipes often say just "bay leaf" and either works.
