# Ghee

Butter cooked until the water is gone and the milk solids toast, then strained. Nutty, high smoke point, shelf-stable. The default fat for Indian tadka. Sold in jars (it's its own purchase, not [[butter]]).

**Cuisines:** [[indian]]

## Substitutes
- [[neutral-oil]]: 1:1, loses the nutty dairy note; makes the dish vegan
