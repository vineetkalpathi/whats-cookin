# Sweet Paprika

Ground dried sweet red peppers. Mild, faintly fruity, and mostly there for deep red colour. The plain "paprika" jar in a US supermarket is this.

Not interchangeable with [[smoked-paprika]], which is dried over oak and tastes of it, and distinct again from hot paprika.

**Cuisines:** [[mediterranean]], [[middle-eastern]]

## Choosing & storing
Paprika fades fast - buy small jars and replace yearly. It should smell sweet and look bright red, not brown. Keep it away from the stove; light and heat are what kill it.

## Prep notes
Bloom it in fat with the other ground spices ([[blooming-spices]]), but keep the heat moderate: paprika has enough sugar to scorch and turn bitter.

## Substitutes
- [[smoked-paprika]]: 1:1, but it brings smoke the dish may not want - start with half
- [[kashmiri-chili-powder]]: 1:1, similar colour, mild but not sweet
