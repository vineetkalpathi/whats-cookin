# Mushroom

White button or cremini, sold by the carton - one small carton is about 8 oz. Cremini are the same species as button, just older, and taste a little deeper.

Mushrooms are the main source of savoury depth in otherwise plain vegetable sautes.

**Cuisines:** [[italian]]

## Choosing & storing
Dry, firm caps with no slick patches. Store in the paper bag, not sealed plastic - trapped moisture makes them slimy.

## Prep notes
Wipe rather than soak. They are mostly water, so they release a lot of it: give them space and heat or they stew. In [[bean-filling]] and [[veggie-pasta]] they go in with the other vegetables, which is why those recipes make so much of using high heat to drive the water off.
