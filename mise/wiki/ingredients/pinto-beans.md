# Pinto Beans

The default bean of northern Mexican and Tex-Mex cooking, and what refried beans are normally made from. Softer and creamier than [[black-beans]], with a mild earthy flavour, and they break down more readily.

**Cuisines:** [[mexican]]

## Choosing & storing
Canned is fine for anything where the beans are one component among many. Drain and rinse to get rid of the thick, salty canning liquid.

## Prep notes
Offered as the alternative to black beans in [[bean-filling]]. Because they soften faster, add them a little later or stir more gently if you want them whole.

## Substitutes
- [[black-beans]]: 1:1, firmer and holds its shape better; the usual swap
