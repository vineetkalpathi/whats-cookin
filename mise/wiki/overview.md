# Overview

The evolving big picture of this kitchen: what gets cooked, recurring themes, and gaps. The agent revises this page when an ingest or query changes the picture.

## Current state
- 9 cuisines and 26 recipes. Nearly everything is sourced now; the only seed pages left are [[black-bean-tacos]] and [[pasta-aglio-e-olio]].
- **Amma's recipes are the backbone of this wiki.** All 17 PDFs are now ingested, giving 18 of the 26 recipes. They are not one cuisine: Indian home cooking ([[yellow-moong-khichdi]], [[methi-lagaan]], [[killu-bonda]]), Thai from jarred paste, Russian [[borscht]], and a run of American blog-style salads.
- **The recurring move across her recipes is an aromatic order, not a spice blend.** Oil, then garlic for a few seconds, then chili, then onion, then everything else - it opens [[bean-filling]], [[veggie-pasta]], [[thai-curry]] and even [[asparagus-soup]], which is otherwise a Western cream soup. [[saute-to-bite]] and [[frying-curry-paste]] are the two techniques that pattern turns into.
- **Her recipes mostly don't measure.** Eleven of the sixteen new ones state no yield, and several give no quantities at all. Where the wiki had to infer amounts ([[thai-curry]], [[asian-cabbage-salad]]) the page says so in a warning callout - worth trusting the method and your own eye over the numbers.
- [[indian]] is the deepest: two chole recipes ([[chana-masala]], [[instant-pot-chana-masala]]) that differ in method and spicing. The user considers them the same dish but wants to keep both recipes as they are for now (2026-11-14).
- **Tofu is the main protein** outside legumes ([[extra-firm-tofu]], [[silken-tofu]]); the user substitutes it for meat, and [[soy-sauce]] for [[fish-sauce]] (see [[thai-tofu-salad]]).
- Shared backbone: [[garlic]], [[onion]], [[cilantro]] and [[neutral-oil]] appear across cuisines. [[skillet]] and [[instant-pot]] are the workhorses; [[blooming-spices]] and [[pressure-cooking]] are the most-used techniques.
- **A vegetable set runs through everything:** [[bell-pepper]], [[broccoli]], [[zucchini]], [[carrot]], [[mushroom]], with [[pickled-jalapenos]] for acid and heat. Cabbage, beets and squash joined it with the soups and salads.
- **The pantry is now genuinely South Asian.** [[indian-grocery]] stocks 16 confirmed specialty items - the dals, [[rava]], [[besan]], [[amchur]], [[curry-leaves]], [[tamarind]] - which is a real shopping constraint the grocery list now knows about.
- Every course is covered now: breakfast ([[shakshuka]]), sides, components, a dessert ([[almond-flour-cake]]) and a run of make-ahead salads and soups.

- House recipes still needing a first draft: chili, kimchi fried rice, shawarma tofu. ([[bean-filling]] and [[veggie-pasta]] were answered by Amma's PDFs instead of a draft.)
- **The user adapts [[shakshuka]]**; their changes still need to be recorded on the recipe page.
- Spices still with no pages: cardamom, Mexican oregano, sumac, za'atar, caraway.
- Thin spots now: no breads or doughs made from scratch, one dessert, and [[american]] is a catch-all that should be split once there are more Western recipes to split it by.
- Eleven recipes have estimated servings, which makes `grocery-list` scaling approximate for them.
- Store availability is unconfirmed. Record findings after each trip.
