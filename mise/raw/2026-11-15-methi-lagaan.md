### p. 1

Methi Lagaan (Jyoti)

**Ingredients:**

- 1/2 cup Rava
- 1/4 cup Besan
- 1/4 cup Maida or Wheat flour (tastes better with maida)
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt (Swad Anusar)
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 tsp green chilies (very finely chopped)
- 2/3 pods of garlic grated
- 1/4 cup corn (thawed)
- 1/2 cup grated carrots
- 1 1/2 cups of methi or palak leaves finely chopped (frozen or fresh).
- 1 small onion (finely chopped)
- 11/2 tbsp sesame seeds (roasted) (split it into two parts)
- 1 tsp of chili powder or as needed

**Materials:**

- Baking dish
- Mixing Bowl
- Apron

1. Pre-heat the oven to 350°F
2. Mix the vegetables (including the cut onion) in the bowl
3. Add the salt and the rava, and mix well
4. Add the besan and maida/flour, and mix well
5. When well mixed, add the milk and oil; mix until consistent
6. Add half of the sesame, sugar, and more salt (as needed)
7. Add both the baking soda and baking powder, and mix well
8. When well mixed, pour the mixture into a well-greased baking pan
9. Top with sesame to decorate, and place in the oven. Let bake for 35-40 minutes
10. Serve hot with chutney and/or ketchup
