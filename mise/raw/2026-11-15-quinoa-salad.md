QINOA SALAD

Servings | Prep Time | Total Time

*(the three header fields are an unfilled template — no values were ever entered)*

INGREDIENTS

- Zucchini
- Onion
- Cucumber
- Celery
- Grated carrot (or slightly cooked)
- Colored peppers
- Canned Chickpeas
- Avocado
- Dried cranberries
- Feta cheese
- Around ½  cup cooked qinoa
- Parsley leaves/dill

Dressing (mix in bottle and shake before applying)

- Olive oil (around 4 tbs)
- Lemon juice (around 1 tbs)
- Honey mustard
- Black pepper
- Salt
- Red crushed pepper
- Some cumin powder (jeera powder)
- Some garlic powder

DIRECTIONS

1. Cook qinoa
2. While qinoa is cooking, cut vegetables to desired size
3. Mix salad in a bowl
4. Mix dressing in a bottle and shake. Apply to salad
