### p. 1

**Bean Filling (for quesadillas/enchiladas/Mexican pasta)**

Ingredients:

- Black or Pinto beans (organic)-15 oz can
- Onion, 1 medium sized
- Garlic, 5 cloves
- Other veggies of your choice, like:
  - Colored peppers, 0.5-1
  - Broccoli, 1 medium sized crown
  - Zucchini, 1
  - Carrot, 1
  - Mushroom, 1 small carton
  - Pickled jalapenos
- Roasted Cumin/Gira powder, ~1 tsp
- Crushed red pepper
- Sriracha
- Black pepper
- Taco powder/seasoning (optional), ~1 tsp
- Chopped coriander, ~5 stems

1. Cut and wash vegetables, wash any canned beans

   Chop vegetables to approximately bean size (or another desired size). Separate onion and
   garlic and wash the rest of the veggies. Pour canned beans into colander and wash until
   juice is removed.

2. Saute/cook vegetables and beans

   Heat 3 tbs of oil in a pan. Add garlic. After 3-5s, add onions. Once onions are slightly
   translucent, add the washed vegetables to the pan and cook to desired "bite"/crunch. Also
   add beans and pickled jalapenos (along with 2-3 spoons of the jalapeno jar vinegar).

   For a crunchier bite, use high heat to evaporate water quickly. For softer bite, use
   slightly lower heat to evaporate water while allowing veggies to cook more. Covering the
   pan with a lid will allow more water retention and result in softer veggies.

*(photos: can of organic black beans; bowl of chopped veg and mushrooms; pan of cooked filling)*

### p. 2

3. Add cumin powder, black pepper, red crushed pepper, additional spices, and chopped coriander

   Add amount according to taste. Top off with chopped coriander and mix.

4. Store in fridge for use with quesadillas, enchiladas, or Mexican pasta
