### p. 1

Asparagus Soup!

1. Cut asparagus:
    1.1. Discard soft tips/hard stem edges
    1.2. Cut each piece into 4
2. Cut 1 medium-sized onion (doesn't have to be fine)
3. Cut 5-6 cloves of garlic (fine-ish)
4. Cut 1 green chili
5. In large pan or InstaPot in sauté mode,
    5.1. Add 2-3 spoons of oil
    5.2. Add garlic (wait 2-3 seconds for sizzle), then chili, then onion
    5.3. When onion is translucent, add 1 tsp of all-purpose flour, cook around 1 min until flour blends in
    5.4. Add asparagus
    5.5. Add EITHER:
        5.5.1. 2 cups vegetable broth (if more liquid needed, then add water)
        5.5.2. Or
        5.5.3. 2 cups of water and a big tablespoon of veggie bullion paste
    5.6. Add salt as needed
6. Allow liquid to boil until asparagus is tender
7. Then blend/add milk
