### p. 1

Tomato-Mozzarella Panini

Ingredients:

- Ciabatta/Torta/other thick bread
- Tomatoes
- Jalapenos
- Mozzarella Cheese
- Butter
- Green Chutney/Pesto
- Black Pepper + Salt
- Red Chili Flakes

*Photo at upper right: a plate with four pressed panini halves showing dark grill marks, served beside a glass jar of orange-red soup.*

1. Cut tomato(es) into circles and mozzarella into circles
    a. Cut jalapenos if not already pickled
2. Cut bread, spread butter on the outsides and chutney/pesto on the insides
3. Layer tomatoes, mozzarella, and jalapenos inside bread
4. Sprinkle salt, pepper and chili flakes
5. Close with other side and toast in panini maker

*Photo below the steps: an open ciabatta loaf on a green cutting board — the top half spread with green chutney/pesto, the bottom half layered with cherry tomato halves, sliced jalapenos and a round of fresh mozzarella; two more mozzarella balls sit on the board, one sprinkled with chili flakes.*
