### p. 1

Thai Curry

Ingredients:

- Thai kitchen paste (red or green)
- Broccoli
- Cauliflower
- Capsicum
- Zucchini
- Mushroom
- Onion
- Baby corn
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Mint
- Green chilli for spice
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- ½ tsp pepper
- Salt
- 1 tbs soy sauce (for larger quantity)
- Tofu
- Coconut milk + coconut cream
- Coriander leaves

*Photos, top right of the ingredient list: three images side by side — a bowl of cut broccoli, cauliflower, carrot and capsicum; a plate of sliced mushrooms and cut baby corn; a package of "Organic Tofu" with Korean lettering (유기농두부) on the label.*

*Photo, below those: a cutting board with a halved red onion, quartered red/purple cabbage[?] or onion, peeled garlic cloves, a knob of ginger, and green beans[?] with cilantro leaves.*

1. For higher volumes of vegetables (Broccoli, Cauliflower, Capsicum, Zucchini), cook slightly ahead of time (medium-high heat); eventually add a *little* salt
    a. For lower volumes, add to saucepan raw during step 4
2. Grate garlic, cut onion, baby corn, mushroom, green chili, mint
3. Prepare saucepan with oil on high heat and sauté green chili, garlic/ginger, onion, mint (put in that order). Can add cumin/gira powder and pepper. Once cooked/shiny lookin, add curry paste/water
4. Combine vegetables with saucepan contents to let vegetables soak in the gravy, add red chili powder for spice if needed
5. Cut tofu and add to the gravy. Cover saucepan and let sit/boil on hgh heat for a few minutes
6. Add coconut milk/cream and let cook for a little longer
7. Add coriander leaves on top

*Photo, right of steps 4–7: the finished curry simmering in a pot — pale coconut gravy with tofu cubes, broccoli and green herbs.*
