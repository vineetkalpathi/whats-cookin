### p. 1

Borcht – Russian Soup (Kumar)

- 2-4 tablespoons olive/vegetable oil
- 2 quarts/~8 cups of vegetable broth (or meat broth)
- 1 large onion, chopped
- One tomato
- 2-3 cloves of garlic, minced
- Veg bullion 2 tsp
- Very small can of tomato paste (2-3 tbs)
- 2 strands of leek/spring onions, chopped
- 2 stubs of celery (*optional: chopped cubes*)
- 1 large russet potato, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
- 2 large beets, peeled and shredded
- 2 carrots, peeled and shredded
- 2 cups of thinly sliced cabbage
- Chopped Dill (to put in soup and sprinkle as garnish)
- 3-4 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 cup (8oz) sour cream
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt to taste
- 1-2 tablespoons black pepper (freshly ground)
- Crushed red pepper
- Black bread/Rye/pumpernickel/other artisan bread of choice

*Photo at upper right: mise en place on a counter — bottle of oil, jars and cartons of broth/bouillon and sour cream, a whole onion and tomato, and a large round tray holding bowls of shredded carrot, chopped celery, chopped leek/spring onion, chopped white onion and a big mound of shredded raw beet.*

In oil add the garlic, tej patta and onion. The one different thing is to caramelize the onions. So cook them till slightly brown. Then add the 3 tbs of tomato paste. When this mix of onion and tomato leaves oil add the potatoes, carrot and celery. When the potato is like 30% cooked add the cabbage and sp onions half of dill and beets. Mix all together and saute fr another 5 mins then add the veg broth or veg bullions and water. When it comes to a boil put a lid and let it simmer. Taste and add the salt. The veg broth has some salt so add accordingly. Cook the vegs to a bite consistency. (Or however u like it). Now add 2/3 tbs of the red wine vinegar and the crushed black pepper and let it boil fr a min or two. Turn off the gas. When serving serve with a dollop of sour cream in the middle and a dill sprinkled on top. If u like it spicy add some crushed red pepper when u sauté the vegs. serve with toasted and buttered black bread or pumpernickel bread
