## Transcription

### p. 1

Killu Bonda

Ingredients

**Bonda:**

- ½ cup wheat flour
- ¼ cup all purpose flour
- ¼ cup rice flour
- 1/3 cup besan
- 2 scoops of idli batter, OR
  - 1/3 cup all purpose flour
  - 1/3 cup rice flour
- 4/3 cup shredded cabbage, carrot, finely cut bell pepper
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch of ginger, grated
- 1 tbs of chilies, finely cut (or as needed)
- A pinch of hing
- Finely cut cilantro and curry leaves
- Salt as needed
- ½ spoon of Baking soda or eno salt
- Oil for frying

**Chutney:**

- 1 tsp Oil
- 1 tbs Urad Dal
- 1 tbs Channa Dal
- 3 or 4 Dried red chili
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 clove garlic
- 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
- Small piece of tamarind
- Salt as needed
