### p. 1

Paneer Kathi Rolls

Ingredients (6 servings):

*(photo, upper right: styled overhead shot — a bowl of cooked paneer-and-pepper filling, a bowl of green chutney, three folded kathi rolls on a plate, a green "Kawan PARATHA" package, a bowl of sliced red onion and a bowl of white sauce/yogurt, on a striped cloth over marble)*

- 2 cups Paneer (~1in cubes)
- 1-2 Mixed Bell peppers (thinly sliced)
- 1 medium Red onion (thinly sliced)
- Marinade ingredients:
  - ¼ cup plain yogurt
  - 1 tsp ginger (grated)
  - 1 tsp garlic (pressed)
  - 1-2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  - 1 tsp garam masala
  - ¼ tsp ground turmeric
  - 1 tsp roasted ground gira
  - 1 tsp ground coriander seeds
  - 1 tsp amchur
  - 1 tbsp kasoori methi
  - 1 Sliced green chili
  - 1 tsp salt
- Serving ingredients:
  - 6-8 Kawan (or any) paratha
  - ½ cup cilantro (chopped)
  - Green chutney
  - Mitha (sweet) chutney

1. Marinate paneer

   Quickly sauté green chili, grated garlic and ginger. Mix all marinade ingredients in a bowl, add paneer and mix well

2. Make filling

   Heat 2 tbs of oil in a pan. Add garlic, onions, peppers, and ½ teaspoon of salt and cook for 3 to 4 minutes. Add marinated paneer and mix well. Cook for another 3 to 4 minutes. Turn the heat off and garnish with cilantro.

3. Cook parathas

4. Assemble Kathi roll

### p. 2

Spread chutney on paratha, add 2-3 spoonfuls of filling in the middle, add few sliced onions and cilantro, roll
