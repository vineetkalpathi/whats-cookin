### p. 1

**Pasta Gravy**

Ingredients:

- Onion, 1 medium sized
- Garlic, 5 cloves
- Other veggies of your choice, like:
  - Colored peppers, 0.5-1
  - Broccoli, 1 medium sized crown
  - Zucchini, 1
  - Carrot, 1
  - Mushroom, 1 small carton
  - Pickled jalapenos
- Crushed red pepper
- Black pepper
- Pasta sauce of your choice (red, pesto, etc.)

1. Cut and wash vegetables

   Chop vegetables to approximately bite size (or another desired size). Separate onion and
   garlic and wash the rest of the veggies.

2. Saute/cook vegetables

   Heat 3 tbs of oil in a pan. Add garlic. After 3-5s, add onions. Once onions are slightly
   translucent, add the washed vegetables to the pan and cook to desired "bite"/crunch.

   For a crunchier bite, use high heat to evaporate water quickly. For softer bite, use
   slightly lower heat to evaporate water while allowing veggies to cook more. Covering the
   pan with a lid will allow more water retention and result in softer veggies.

3. Add black pepper, red crushed pepper, salt, additional spices

   Add amount according to taste. Top off with chopped basil and mix.
