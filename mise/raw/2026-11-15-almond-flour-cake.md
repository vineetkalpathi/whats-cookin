Ingredients

-4 large eggs, separated, at room temperature
-1/2 cup castor sugar (124[?] g) + 2 tablespoons for the baking pan.
-1 teaspoon vanilla extract
-1 1/2 cups (142[?] g) almond flour
-1 teaspoon baking powder
-1/4 teaspoon salt
-handful of chocolate chips

Instructions

- Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease an 8" round pan with melted butter. Sprinkle 2 tablespoons of the sugar at the bottom of the pan.
- In a large mixing bowl, beat together the egg yolks,  1/4 cup of the sugar, and the vanilla until smooth.
- Using an electric mixer or stand mixer, whip the egg whites until they form soft peaks.  Slowly beat in half the sugar (1/4 cup). Set aside.
- Combine together the dry ingredients: almond flour, baking powder and salt and add to the egg yolk mixture. Stir together to form a thick dough.
- Take a strong spatual & fold in the egg whites, 1/2 cup at a time, incorporating them fully between each addition. The final addition should result in a smooth, fluffy batter.
- Pour half the cake batter into the prepared pan. Throw in the chocolate chips in the middle. Pour in the rest of the batter.
- Bake the cake on the center rack for 30 minutes, until it's golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
- Remove the cake from the oven and allow it to cool in the pan for 5 minutes. Run a knife around the edge of the pan to loosen the sides, then turn the cake out onto a serving plate.
- Allow the cake to cool fully before topping with chocolate ganache, nutella or sliced fruits. But its lovely as a warm sponge cake with tea
