## Transcription

### p. 1

Yellow Moong Khichidi

Ingredients (2-3 servings):

*(photo, upper right: granite counter with a white spice bottle, a bowl of frozen peas/carrot mix, a tub of rice/moong dal, and a green cutting board holding a plate of ground spices, a cup of mustard seeds, green chilies, a dried red chili, garlic and a red onion)*

- ¾ cup Moong dal
- ¾ cup Rice
- 3-4 green chili
- 1 red chili
- 5 cloves of garlic
- 1 small onion
- 1 tsp jeera/cumin seeds
- 1 tsp kadaga/mustard seeds
- ¼ tsp turmeric poweder
- ¼ tsp Hing/asafetida
- ½ tsp jeera powder
- ½ tsp dhanya/coriander powder
- 1 tsp Kasoori methi/dried methi leaves
- Few Curry leaves
- Optional vegetables
  - Frozen peas/carrot mix (~¾ cup)
  - Spinach

1. Wash rice/moong dal mix; drain and keep aside
2. Wash vegetables and cut

   Chop vegetables; finely cut onion/garlic, cut green chili in half lengthwise, split red chili into 2 over pot so seeds come out

3. Turn InstaPot to sauté mode

   Heat 1 tbs oil and 1 tbs ghee—clarified butter (or 2 tbs oil) in the pot. Add ingredients in the following order:

   a. Kadaga/mustard seeds/rye
   b. Cumin seeds (continue after seeds start to sputter)
   c. Curry leaves and Kasoori methi
   d. All chilis (green and red)
   e. Garlic
   f. Onion
   g. Rice/moong mix

   Mix well.

### p. 2

4. Add spices: Jeera powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, hing. Mix well.
5. Add water. Mix in salt to taste (taste water).

   Generally→ Rice : Water ratio = 1:2

   For khichidi/more mushy add slightly (3/4 cup) more water

6. Turn off sauté mode and close/seal cooker and press "Rice"
