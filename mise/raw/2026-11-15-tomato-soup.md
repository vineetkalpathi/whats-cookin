### p. 1

Tomato Soup

**Ingredients (for 3 bottles worth):**

- ¼ cup yellow moong dal
- 3 medium tomatoes
- 1 green chili (or pepper corns for spice)
- Salt, for taste
- Optional: ½ tsp ghee (clarified butter), roasted cumin seed powder

1. Empty moong dal into skillet, dry roast for 2-3 mins
   a. Dry roasting is to get nice aroma
2. Rinse/wash dal after roasting
3. Put dal back into skillet, add 2 cups of water, little salt, and let water boil so dal becomes soft
4. When dal is ¾ soft, add cut tomatoes to dal
5. Add green chili/pepper, let it all boil until tomato and dal is cooked
6. Keep adding water to make sure ingredients are still submerged
7. Once cooked, let cool and use blender to blend into smooth soup
8. Add water to adjust consistency, salt to adjust taste
9. Optional: ½ tsp ghee and roasted cumin seed powder on top
