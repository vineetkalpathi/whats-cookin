# Traditional Chana Masala Recipe (Chole)

Make restaurant-style Chana Masala right at home with this easy and delicious recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dry chickpeas (See notes for canned chickpeas)
- 4 cups water (divided )
- 1 teaspoon Kosher Salt
- 1 black tea bag (optional)
- 2 tablespoons ghee (or oil )
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 large yellow onion (finely diced)
- 2 teaspoons ginger paste
- 2 teaspoons garlic paste
- 1 green chilli (slit in the middle, optional )
- 2 plum tomatoes (finely diced )
- 1 to 2 tablespoons chana masala spice blend (add more to taste (see notes))
- 1½ teaspoon kosher salt
- ¼ teaspooon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon kashmiri red chili powder
- 1 cup water
- 1 tablespoon kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- ¼ cup chopped cilantro

## Instructions

1. Soak white chickpeas in 2 cups of water overnight or for 8-10 hours. Drain out all the water.
2. Add chickpeas along with 2 cups of water and salt to Instant Pot insert or a stove top pressure cooker. Pressure cook for 35 minutes followed by natural pressure release. (Note: Optionally, add a tea bag to give the dish a rich smoky flavor and deep color. Discard once the chickpeas are cooked)
3. Heat ghee in a medium skillet. Add bay leaf and onions. Sauté on medium heat until the onions turn light golden brown, about 8 to 10 minutes. Use a lid to speed up cooking while mixing in the onions frequently. Add ginger paste, garlic paste, and green chili. Mix well and cook for another minute.
4. Add tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes stirring a few times until the tomatoes are soft and mushy.
5. Mix well with chana masala spice blend, salt, turmeric, and red chili powder. Add boiled chickpeas along with the water from pressure cooking. Add one cup of water(more or less) to bring the curry to your desired consistency.
6. Simmer for 5 minutes and garnish with Kasoori methi and cilantro. Serve with steamed basmati rice or paratha.
