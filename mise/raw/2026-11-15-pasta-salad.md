INGREDIENTS

- Zucchini
- Onions
- corn
- Colored peppers
- Basil leaves
- Carrot/broccoli (quick boil ~ 2 min)
- Pasta
- Mayo
- Olive oil
- Lemon juice
- Black pepper
- Salt
- Red crushed pepper

DIRECTIONS

1. Cook pasta
2. While pasta is cooking, cut vegetables to desired size. If using carrot/broccoli, boil quickly to soften slightly (around 2 min).
3. Mix everything in a bowl, add basil, olive oil, lemon juice, black pepper, salt, red crushed pepper
4. Mix in mayo and onions once ready to serve (gets watery and gross if you add and store overnight)
